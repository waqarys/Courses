## Description

How do you turn words into numbers that carry meaning? This walkthrough covers why ASCII codes and word IDs fail, words as points in space, Euclidean distance vs cosine similarity, why embeddings need hundreds of dimensions, king − man + woman ≈ queen (tested on real GloVe vectors — 4 of the book's 7 examples hit top-1), how Word2Vec learns from context, static vs contextual embeddings, and why averaging word vectors loses word order.

This is part of my study series on the book "Build Your First LLM: A Hands-On Guide to Language Models" by Hasan Degismez. All slides, narration and diagrams are my own teaching adaptation. Please support the author and buy the book: https://leanpub.com/FirstLLM/
