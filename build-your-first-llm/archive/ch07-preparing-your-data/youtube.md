## Description

Part III begins with the data. We build a complete, repeatable pipeline: sourcing text ethically, cleaning and Unicode normalisation, exact deduplication with hashes, leak-free train/val/test splits by document, overlapping chunks for the context window, JSONL storage and sanity checks. Real CPU outputs from the book's ch07 notebook, plus my own experiment pushing three public-domain H. G. Wells novels through the pipeline (chars per token, duplicates, chunk sizes), solved exercises, and three book corrections.

This is part of my study series on the book "Build Your First LLM: A Hands-On Guide to Language Models" by Hasan Degismez. All slides, narration and diagrams are my own teaching adaptation. Please support the author and buy the book: https://leanpub.com/FirstLLM/
