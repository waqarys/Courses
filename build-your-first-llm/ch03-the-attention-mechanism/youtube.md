## Description

Attention is the idea that made modern LLMs possible. This walkthrough covers the RNN sequential bottleneck, attention as selective focus, Query/Key/Value (the library analogy), the full calculation by hand (scores → softmax → weighted sum) and why we divide by √d, multi-head attention, self- vs cross-attention, causal masking, the n² cost, and the 2017 "Attention Is All You Need" paper. Then we look inside real GPT-2 attention maps — attention sinks, causal masking, and why the classic pronoun exercise needs a bidirectional model.

This is part of my study series on the book "Build Your First LLM: A Hands-On Guide to Language Models" by Hasan Degismez. All slides, narration and diagrams are my own teaching adaptation. Please support the author and buy the book: https://leanpub.com/FirstLLM/
