## Description
Training is hiking downhill in fog: you can only feel the slope under your feet (the gradient), so you take small steps (learning rate), warm up slowly, and check a map you didn't train on (validation) to know you're not lost.

In this module of *Zero to LLM* we build it ourselves in plain Python and PyTorch — every line explained.
