## Description

Attention alone isn't a model. This walkthrough assembles the complete Transformer: position encodings (sinusoidal, learned, RoPE — with GPT-2's real position table), feed-forward networks and GELU, residual connections, LayerNorm/RMSNorm and Pre- vs Post-Norm, one full layer and the stacked model, encoder vs decoder (GPT vs BERT), training vs inference, and where GPT-2's 124M parameters actually live. Includes three book corrections (LayerNorm example, GELU/SwiGLU, GPT-4/RoPE).

This is part of my study series on the book "Build Your First LLM: A Hands-On Guide to Language Models" by Hasan Degismez. All slides, narration and diagrams are my own teaching adaptation. Please support the author and buy the book: https://leanpub.com/FirstLLM/
