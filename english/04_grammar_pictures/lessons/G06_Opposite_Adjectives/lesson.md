# 60 Opposite Adjectives

## INTRO
Welcome to Verb Forms and Grammar with Pictures. Today, sixty adjectives, in thirty pairs of opposites.
Learning words in pairs really helps: when you remember one, you remember both.
For each pair, look at the pictures, listen, and repeat both words. Then hear them in a real conversation.

## WORDS | Opposites 1 to 30
@ word hot – cold=hot, cold | 🔥 🧊 | hot is the opposite of cold | Is the soup hot? | Yes, careful! It's very hot.
@ word big – small=big, small | 🐘 🐭 | big is the opposite of small | Is your flat big? | No, it's quite small.
@ word fast – slow=fast, slow | 🐆 🐢 | fast is the opposite of slow | Is the internet fast at your place? | No, it's really slow today.
@ word old – new=old, new | 📻 📱 | old is the opposite of new | Is that a new phone? | Yes, my old one broke.
@ word young – old=young, old | 👶 👵 | young is the opposite of old | How old is your grandmother? | She's ninety, but she feels young!
@ word happy – sad=happy, sad | 😄 😢 | happy is the opposite of sad | Why are you so happy? | I passed my driving test!
@ word full – empty=full, empty | 🥛 🥃 | full is the opposite of empty | Is there any milk? | No, the bottle is empty.
@ word clean – dirty=clean, dirty | 🧼 🦠 | clean is the opposite of dirty | Are these plates clean? | No, they're dirty. Put them in the sink.
@ word heavy – light=heavy, light | 🏋️ 🪶 | heavy is the opposite of light | Is your bag heavy? | No, it's very light.
@ word loud – quiet=loud, quiet | 📢 🤫 | loud is the opposite of quiet | Is your street loud at night? | No, it's quiet after ten.
@ word rich – poor=rich, poor | 💰 🪙 | rich is the opposite of poor | What would you do if you were rich? | I'd travel the world.
@ word strong – weak=strong, weak | 💪 🥱 | strong is the opposite of weak | How's your Wi-Fi signal? | It's weak in the bedroom.
@ word hard – soft=hard, soft | 🪨 🧸 | hard is the opposite of soft | Is the bed comfortable? | Yes, but the pillow is too hard.
@ word wet – dry=wet, dry | 🌧️ 🏜️ | wet is the opposite of dry | Is the washing dry yet? | No, it's still wet.
@ word open – closed=open, closed | 🔓 🔒 | open is the opposite of closed | Is the pharmacy open? | No, it's closed on Sundays.

## REVIEW | Review: say both words
@ review happy – sad, full – empty, clean – dirty, heavy – light, loud – quiet, rich – poor, strong – weak, hard – soft | 10

## WORDS | Opposites 31 to 60
@ word early – late=early, late | 🌅 🌙 | early is the opposite of late | Are we late? | No, we're ten minutes early.
@ word expensive – cheap=expensive, cheap | 💎 🏷️ | expensive is the opposite of cheap | Was the jacket expensive? | No, it was really cheap in the sale.
@ word easy – difficult=easy, difficult | ✅ 🧩 | easy is the opposite of difficult | Was the exam difficult? | No, it was easier than I expected.
@ word tall – short=tall, short | 🦒 🐧 | tall is the opposite of short | Is your brother tall? | Yes, he's taller than me.
@ word long – short=long, short | 🐍 🐛 | long is the opposite of short | Was the meeting long? | No, it was short, only twenty minutes.
@ word thick – thin=thick, thin | 📚 📄 | thick is the opposite of thin | Is the book long? | Yes, it's very thick.
@ word wide – narrow=wide, narrow | 🛣️ 🚶 | wide is the opposite of narrow | Can a bus get down that street? | No, it's too narrow.
@ word deep – shallow=deep, shallow | 🌊 🏖️ | deep is the opposite of shallow | Is the pool deep? | Only at one end. The other end is shallow.
@ word sweet – sour=sweet, sour | 🍰 🍋 | sweet is the opposite of sour | How's the lemonade? | It's a bit sour. Add some sugar.
@ word safe – dangerous=safe, dangerous | 🦺 ⚠️ | safe is the opposite of dangerous | Is it safe to swim here? | No, the current is dangerous.
@ word busy – free=busy, free | 📅 🏖️ | busy is the opposite of free | Are you free on Friday? | Sorry, I'm really busy this week.
@ word right – wrong=right, wrong | ✔️ ❌ | right is the opposite of wrong | Is this the right answer? | No, I think it's wrong.
@ word tidy – messy=tidy, messy | 🧺 🌪️ | tidy is the opposite of messy | Is your desk tidy? | Not really. It's quite messy.
@ word polite – rude=polite, rude | 🙇 😤 | polite is the opposite of rude | Was the waiter polite? | Yes, very polite and friendly.
@ word brave – scared=brave, scared | 🦁 😱 | brave is the opposite of scared | Are you scared of spiders? | A little. My sister is braver than me.

## QUIZ | Quick quiz: what's the opposite?
@ q [🌊] The opposite of deep is... | narrow | *shallow | short
Shallow. A shallow pool is not deep.
@ q [🧩] The opposite of easy is... | heavy | *difficult | busy
Difficult. Hard is also correct.
@ q [🙇] The opposite of polite is... | *rude | brave | messy
Rude.
@ q [🧺] The opposite of messy is... | clean | *tidy | free
Tidy. A clean room has no dirt; a tidy room has everything in its place.
@ q [📄] The opposite of thick is... | *thin | short | light
Thin.
@ q [🪶] Light can have two opposites. Which pair is right? | heavy and big | *heavy and dark | dark and small
Heavy, for weight: a light bag, a heavy bag. And dark, for colour: a light room, a dark room.
