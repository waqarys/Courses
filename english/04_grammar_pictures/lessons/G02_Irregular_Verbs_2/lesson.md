# 50 Irregular Verbs, Part 2

## INTRO
Welcome back to Verb Forms and Grammar with Pictures. This is Part 2: fifty more irregular verbs.
Same method: look at the picture, listen, repeat the three forms out loud, then hear a real sentence.
At the end, there's a group of verbs that never change at all. Let's go.

## VERBS | Verbs 76 to 100
@ verb freeze | froze | frozen | 🥶 | The lake froze last winter.
@ verb wake | woke | woken | ⏰ | I woke up at five.
@ verb forgive | forgave | forgiven | 🫂 | She forgave him for being late.
@ verb lie | lay | lain | 🛌 | I lay on the sofa all afternoon.
Lie, lay, lain means to rest your body. Be careful: lay is also a different verb.
@ verb lay | laid | laid | 🍽️ | He laid the plates on the table.
Lay, laid, laid means to put something down. So: I lay down, but I laid the table.
@ verb lead | led | led | 🧭 | She led the team to victory.
@ verb light | lit | lit | 🕯️ | We lit candles at dinner.
@ verb mean | meant | meant | ❓ | Sorry, I meant Tuesday, not Thursday.
@ verb bend | bent | bent | 🙇 | He bent down to pick up the coin.
@ verb burn | burnt / burned | burnt / burned | 🔥 | I burnt the toast again.
Both forms are correct. Burned is more common in American English.
@ verb become | became | become | 🦋 | She became a doctor at twenty-six.
@ verb seek | sought | sought | 🔎 | They sought help from a lawyer.
@ verb swear | swore | sworn | 🤬 | He swore he was telling the truth.
@ verb strike | struck | struck | ⚡ | Lightning struck the tree.
@ verb bleed | bled | bled | 🩸 | My finger bled a little.
@ verb slide | slid | slid | 🛝 | The car slid on the ice.
@ verb deal | dealt | dealt | 🃏 | She dealt the cards.
@ verb bet | bet | bet | 🎰 | I bet you can't do it!
These next verbs are easy: all three forms are the same.
@ verb cut | cut | cut | ✂️ | I cut my hair last week.
@ verb hit | hit | hit | 🔨 | He hit his thumb with a hammer.
@ verb hurt | hurt | hurt | 🤕 | I hurt my back at the gym.
@ verb shut | shut | shut | 🚪 | She shut the door quietly.
@ verb cost | cost | cost | 💰 | The tickets cost fifty dollars.
@ verb set | set | set | ⏲️ | I set an alarm for six.
@ verb split | split | split | 🍕 | We split the pizza four ways.

## PRACTICE | Final check
@ fix Yesterday I laid on the sofa all day. => Yesterday I lay on the sofa all day.
To rest your body is lie, lay, lain. Laid is from lay, to put something down.
@ fill The pipes ___ (freeze) last night. | froze
@ fill I've ___ (cut) my finger. | cut
@ fill Lightning ___ (strike) the old tower. | struck
@ fix She has became a manager. => She has become a manager.
After has, use V3: become.
