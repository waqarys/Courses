# Comparatives & Superlatives

## INTRO
Welcome to Verb Forms and Grammar with Pictures. Today: comparatives and superlatives.
We use them to compare things. An elephant is bigger than a horse. The blue whale is the biggest animal in the world.
First, four short rules. Then thirty adjectives with pictures to listen and repeat.

## STUDY | Four rules
@ table Adjective | Rule | Comparative | Superlative ; short: tall | + er / + est | taller | the tallest ; ends in e: nice | + r / + st | nicer | the nicest ; vowel + consonant: big | double + er | bigger | the biggest ; ends in y: easy | y: ier / iest | easier | the easiest ; long: expensive | more / most | more expensive | the most expensive
@ point Comparative + **than**: My brother is **taller than** me.
@ point Superlative with **the**: She's **the tallest** person in our family.
Use than with comparatives, and the with superlatives.

## VERBS | Short adjectives: + er, + est
@ cols Adjective | Comparative | Superlative
@ verb tall | taller | the tallest | 🦒 | A giraffe is taller than a man.
@ verb small | smaller | the smallest | 🐭 | A mouse is smaller than a cat.
@ verb fast | faster | the fastest | 🐆 | The cheetah is the fastest animal on land.
@ verb cold | colder | the coldest | 🥶 | January is the coldest month here.
@ verb old | older | the oldest | 👴 | My grandfather is the oldest person in our family.
@ verb cheap | cheaper | the cheapest | 🏷️ | The bus is cheaper than a taxi.
@ verb nice | nicer | the nicest | 🌸 | This café is nicer than the one near work.
@ verb safe | safer | the safest | 🦺 | Trains are safer than cars.
@ verb late | later | the latest | ⏰ | Let's meet later, at eight.

## VERBS | Double the last letter
@ verb big | bigger | the biggest | 🐘 | An elephant is bigger than a horse.
@ verb hot | hotter | the hottest | 🔥 | July is the hottest month of the year.
@ verb thin | thinner | the thinnest | 📏 | This phone is thinner than my old one.
@ verb fat | fatter | the fattest | 🐷 | Our cat is fatter than the neighbour's cat.
@ verb sad | sadder | the saddest | 😢 | It's the saddest film I've ever seen.
@ verb wet | wetter | the wettest | 🌧️ | November is the wettest month here.

## VERBS | Ending in y: ier, iest
@ verb easy | easier | the easiest | ✅ | This exercise is easier than the last one.
@ verb happy | happier | the happiest | 😄 | She looks happier since her new job.
@ verb busy | busier | the busiest | 🏙️ | Friday is the busiest day at the restaurant.
@ verb heavy | heavier | the heaviest | 🏋️ | My suitcase is heavier than yours.
@ verb funny | funnier | the funniest | 😂 | He's the funniest person I know.
@ verb early | earlier | the earliest | 🌅 | Can we start earlier tomorrow?

## VERBS | Long adjectives: more, most
@ verb expensive | more expensive | the most expensive | 💎 | Diamonds are more expensive than gold.
@ verb beautiful | more beautiful | the most beautiful | 🏞️ | It's the most beautiful lake in the country.
@ verb dangerous | more dangerous | the most dangerous | 🦈 | Sharks are more dangerous than dolphins.
@ verb comfortable | more comfortable | the most comfortable | 🛋️ | This sofa is more comfortable than the old one.
@ verb interesting | more interesting | the most interesting | 📚 | The book was more interesting than the film.
@ verb popular | more popular | the most popular | ⭐ | Cricket is the most popular sport in India.

## VERBS | Irregular: learn these by heart
@ verb good | better | the best | 🏆 | This is the best pizza in town.
@ verb bad | worse | the worst | 👎 | The traffic is worse than yesterday.
@ verb far | further | the furthest | 🗺️ | Which planet is the furthest from the sun?
You may also hear farther and the farthest. Both are correct.

## PRACTICE | Make the comparison
@ fill A plane is ___ (fast) than a train. | faster
@ fill This is ___ (good) coffee I've ever had. | the best
@ fill My new flat is ___ (big) than my old one. | bigger
@ fill Today is ___ (hot) day of the year. | the hottest
@ fill English is ___ (easy) than I thought. | easier
@ fix My new phone is more better than my old one. => My new phone is better than my old one.
Better is already a comparative. Never say more better.
@ fix She's the most tall person in the team. => She's the tallest person in the team.
Tall is a short adjective, so we add e s t.
@ fix This bag is more expensive that one. => This bag is more expensive than that one.
After a comparative, we use than.
