# Present Simple vs Present Continuous

## INTRO
Welcome to Verb Forms and Grammar with Pictures. Today, two tenses that every learner mixes up: I work, and I'm working.
One is for things that are usually true. The other is for things happening now. Let's see them with pictures.

## STUDY | Present simple: usually, always, every day
@ pic 💼 📅 | Maya **works** in a bank. She **starts** at nine every day.
The present simple is for habits, routines and facts. Things that are generally true.
@ pic 🌍 ☀️ | The sun **rises** in the east. Water **boils** at a hundred degrees.
Facts that are always true use the present simple too.
@ point Form: I / you / we / they **work**. He / she / it **works**. Don't forget the s!
@ point Signal words: **always, usually, often, sometimes, never, every day, on Mondays**.

## STUDY | Present continuous: now, around now
@ pic 🏖️ 📸 | But today is Saturday. Maya **is lying** on the beach. She **isn't working**.
The present continuous is for actions happening right now.
@ pic 📚 🧑‍🎓 | She**'s learning** Spanish this month.
It's also for temporary actions around now, even if she's not doing it at this exact second.
@ point Form: am / is / are + verb + **ing**: I**'m reading**, he**'s cooking**, they**'re waiting**.
@ point Signal words: **now, right now, at the moment, today, this week, look!, listen!**

## STUDY | Verbs we don't use in the continuous
@ table Feelings | Thinking | Having ; like, love, hate, want, need | know, understand, believe, mean | have (own), belong, cost
These are states, not actions. So we say, I know the answer. Not, I'm knowing.
@ point But: I **have** a car (own it). I'**m having** lunch (an action: eating). Both are correct!

## PRACTICE | Simple or continuous?
Say the whole sentence before the answer appears.
@ fill My dad usually ___ (drink) coffee in the morning. | drinks
Usually: a habit, so present simple.
@ fill Shh! The baby ___ (sleep). | is sleeping
Right now, so present continuous.
@ fill I ___ (not understand) this question. | don't understand
Understand is a state verb: no ing.
@ fill Look! It ___ (snow)! | is snowing
Look! means it's happening now.
@ fill She ___ (work) from home this week. | is working
This week: temporary, so continuous.
@ fill Water ___ (freeze) at zero degrees. | freezes
A fact, so present simple.
@ fill We ___ (have) dinner at the moment. Can I call you back? | are having
Having dinner is an action, so it can be continuous.
@ fill How often ___ you ___ (go) to the gym? | do ... go
How often asks about habits: present simple.

## PRACTICE | Spot the mistake
@ fix I am knowing the answer. => I know the answer.
Know is a state verb.
@ fix She work in a hospital. => She works in a hospital.
He, she, it: add s.
@ fix What do you do right now? => What are you doing right now?
Right now: present continuous.
@ fix I'm usually walking to work. => I usually walk to work.
Usually: a habit, so present simple.
@ fix This bag is belonging to my sister. => This bag belongs to my sister.
Belong is a state verb.

## QUIZ | Quick quiz: choose the right form
@ q [☕] I ___ tea. I don't like coffee. | 'm preferring | *prefer | am prefer
Prefer is a state verb: I prefer.
@ q [📱] Can you call back later? I ___ right now. | drive | *'m driving | drives
Right now: present continuous.
@ q [🚌] The bus ___ at seven every morning. | *leaves | is leaving | leave
Every morning: a timetable, so present simple.
@ q [🌧️] Take an umbrella. It ___. | rains | *'s raining | rain
It's happening now.
@ q [🏠] They ___ in Pune, but this year they're staying in Delhi. | are living | *live | lives
Their permanent home: present simple. The temporary situation: continuous.
@ q [🍕] This pizza ___ amazing! | is smelling | *smells | smell
Smell, about how something is, is a state: it smells amazing.
@ q [💭] What ___ about? You look worried. | do you think | *are you thinking | you think
Thinking about something now is an action, so continuous. But, I think it's good, is an opinion: simple.
@ q [⚽] He ___ football every Saturday. | is playing | play | *plays
Every Saturday: a habit. And he takes s.
