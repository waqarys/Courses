# At, On, In: Prepositions of Time

## INTRO
Welcome to Verb Forms and Grammar with Pictures. Today: at, on and in, for time.
There's one simple picture that explains almost everything. Think of a triangle: small at the top, big at the bottom. At is for the smallest, most exact time. On is for days. In is for the biggest periods.
Let's look.

## STUDY | The time triangle
@ pic ⏰ | **at** + an exact time: at seven o'clock, at half past nine, at noon.
At is for a point on the clock.
@ pic 📅 | **on** + a day or a date: on Monday, on my birthday, on the fifth of May.
On is for days and dates.
@ pic ☀️ | **in** + a longer period: in July, in summer, in 2030, in the twenty-first century.
In is for months, seasons, years and longer.

## STUDY | Parts of the day, and the exceptions
@ table in the morning | in the afternoon | in the evening ; at night | at midnight | at lunchtime
We say in the morning, in the afternoon and in the evening. But at night!
@ point **on** Monday **morning**: when a day comes first, use on.
@ point **at** the weekend (British), **on** the weekend (American).
@ point **at** Christmas (the holiday), but **on** Christmas Day (a day).
@ point No preposition before **this, next, last, every**: I'll see you next Monday. Not, on next Monday.

@ fill I often read ___ night. | at

@ fill It's very cold here ___ winter. | in

## PRACTICE | Spot the mistake
@ fix I started this job on 2025. => I started this job in 2025.
Years take in.
@ fix See you on next Friday! => See you next Friday!
No preposition before next.
@ fix We usually go for a walk in Sunday morning. => We usually go for a walk on Sunday morning.
A day comes first, so we use on.
@ fix The train leaves in 6:45. => The train leaves at 6:45.
An exact time takes at.

## PRACTICE | Round 2: quick choice
@ q I'll call you ___ the evening. | at | on | *in
In the evening.
@ q The meeting is ___ the fifteenth. | in | *on | at
On the fifteenth: a date.
@ q Let's meet ___ lunchtime. | in | on | *at
At lunchtime.
@ q I'll finish the project ___ two weeks. | at | on | *in
In two weeks means two weeks from now.
@ q We met ___ a rainy Saturday. | in | *on | at
On a rainy Saturday: still a day.
@ q I'm visiting my family ___ Christmas. | in | on | *at
At Christmas, the holiday period.

## PRACTICE | Round 3: about you
Now longer sentences, like real life. Say the whole sentence out loud.
@ fill I usually get up ___ six thirty ___ weekdays. | at ... on
At six thirty, on weekdays.
@ fill We're going on holiday ___ August, ___ the tenth. | in ... on
In August, on the tenth.
@ fill I work from home ___ Fridays, and I go to the gym ___ the evening. | on ... in
On Fridays, in the evening.
@ fill The shop opens ___ nine ___ the morning. | at ... in
At nine in the morning.
@ fill My parents got married ___ 1995, ___ a sunny day ___ June. | in ... on ... in
In 1995, on a sunny day in June.
