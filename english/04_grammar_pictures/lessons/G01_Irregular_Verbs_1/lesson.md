# 50 Irregular Verbs, Part 1

## INTRO
Welcome to Verb Forms and Grammar with Pictures. Today: fifty irregular verbs you use every day.
For each verb you'll see a picture and hear three forms. Repeat all three out loud, then hear the verb in a real sentence.
Let's start with how the three forms work.

## STUDY | V1, V2, V3: what are they?
@ point **V1**, the base form: I **go** to work every day.
V1 is the base form. We use it in the present simple, and after will, can and to.
@ point **V2**, the past simple: I **went** to work yesterday.
V2 is the past simple. Use it for finished actions in the past.
@ point **V3**, the past participle: I have **gone** to work. The work was **done**.
V3 is the past participle. Use it after have, has and had, and in the passive.
@ table Regular | V1 | V2 | V3 ; walk | walk | walked | walked ; Irregular | go | went | gone
Regular verbs just add e d. Irregular verbs change, so we learn them by heart. And the best way to learn them is to say them out loud, again and again.
