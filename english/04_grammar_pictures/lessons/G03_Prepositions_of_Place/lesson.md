# Prepositions of Place

## INTRO
Welcome to Verb Forms and Grammar with Pictures. Today: prepositions of place. Little words that answer the question, where?
Look at each picture, listen, and repeat. Then you'll hear a real question and answer.
In the second half, we'll do prepositions of movement: words that answer, which way?

## WORDS | Where is it?
@ word in | pos:in:📦:🐈 | inside a container or a space | Where's the cat? | It's in the box.
@ word on | pos:on:🪑:🐈 | touching the top of a surface | Where's the cat? | It's on the chair.
@ word under | pos:under:🪑:🐈 | lower than something, and covered by it | Where are my shoes? | They're under the bed.
@ word behind | pos:behind:📦:🐈 | at the back of something | Where's the car park? | It's behind the supermarket.
@ word in front of | pos:front:🏠:🚗 | facing the front of something | Where did you park? | In front of the house.
@ word next to | pos:next:🏠:🌳 | very close, at the side | Who sits next to you at work? | Ravi sits next to me.
@ word near | pos:near:🏠:🏪 | not far from something | Is there a shop near here? | Yes, there's one near the station.
@ word between | pos:between:🌳:🐈:🌲 | with one thing on each side | Where's the bank? | It's between the café and the post office.
@ word above | pos:above:🛋️:🖼️ | higher than something, not touching | Where's the clock? | It's above the door.
@ word below | pos:below:🖼️:🛋️ | lower than something, not touching | Where's the switch? | It's just below the light.
@ word opposite | pos:opposite:🏠:🏦 | on the other side, facing it | Where's the pharmacy? | It's opposite the bank.
@ word inside | pos:inside:🏠:🐈 | in, especially in a building | Where are the bikes? | They're inside the garage.
@ word outside | pos:outside:🏠:🐕 | not in a building | Where's the dog? | It's outside, in the garden.
@ word against | pos:against:🧱:🚲 | touching and leaning on something | Where's your bike? | It's against the wall.

## STUDY | In, on or at?
@ point **in** a space: in the kitchen, in my bag, in London.
@ point **on** a surface or a line: on the table, on the wall, on the second floor.
@ point **at** a point or a place where something happens: at the bus stop, at work, at the door.
A small tip: we say on the bus and on the train, but in a car and in a taxi.

## PRACTICE | In, on or at?
@ fill My phone is ___ my bag. | in
@ fill I'll meet you ___ the bus stop. | at
@ fill There's a photo ___ the wall. | on
@ fill She's sitting ___ the train. | on
@ fix The keys are in the table. => The keys are on the table.
A table is a surface, so we use on.

## WORDS | Which way? Prepositions of movement
@ word into | pos:into:📦:🐈 | moving to the inside of something | What did the cat do? | It jumped into the box.
@ word onto | pos:onto:🪑:🐈 | moving to the top of something | Where did the cat jump? | Onto the chair.
@ word over | pos:over:🧱:🐈 | from one side to the other, going above | How did the cat get out? | It climbed over the wall.
@ word across | pos:across:🛣️:🚶 | from one side to the other side | Where is he going? | He's walking across the road.
@ word through | pos:through:🚇:🚆 | in one side and out the other | Where does the train go? | It goes through a long tunnel.
@ word around | pos:around:🌳:🏃 | in a circle | What do you do in the morning? | I run around the park.
@ word along | pos:along:🛣️:🚴 | following a line, from one end to the other | Where do you cycle? | Along the river.
@ word past | pos:past:🏠:🚗 | going by something without stopping | Do I need to stop at the school? | No, go past the school.
