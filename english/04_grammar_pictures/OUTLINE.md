# Series 4: Verb Forms & Grammar with Pictures (10 lessons, season 1)

Teen and adult learners, A2-B1. NOT made for kids. Short, clear grammar lessons built from four kinds of segments:
STUDY cards (rules with highlighted examples, tables, pictures), VERBS tables (a picture, the forms appear one by one
and are said, a listen-and-repeat ring, then an example sentence), picture WORDS (prepositions drawn as small scenes,
opposites as picture pairs), and PRACTICE (fill the gap, spot the mistake, multiple choice, each with a 6-second
countdown and a one-line reason). Estimated 7-13 min each (G09 rendered on the box). 30 Shorts (verb chants, quiz
questions, fill-the-gap and spot-the-mistake).
