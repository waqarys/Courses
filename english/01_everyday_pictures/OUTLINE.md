# Series 1: Everyday English with Pictures (16 lessons, season 1)

Teen and adult learners, A2-B1. NOT made for kids. Picture-card lessons: guess the picture (3 s) -> word + listen and
repeat -> meaning -> real question-and-answer example (two voices). A review round of 8 pictures after every 10
words (subscribe ask after review 1), a 10-picture final test ("write your score in the comments"), outro with the
next lesson. Estimated lengths 11-20 min (V01 rendered on the box: 17.1 min). 36 Shorts ("name these 4 in 5 seconds").
