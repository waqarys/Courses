# 40 Things in Your House with Pictures

## INTRO
Welcome to Everyday English with Pictures. Today we walk through a house, room by room, and learn forty things you see every day.
Guess the picture, listen, and repeat. Each word also comes with a short question and answer, the kind you hear in real homes.
Get ready to keep your score. Let's open the front door.

## WORDS | The hall and the living room
@ word front door | 🚪 | the main door you use to enter a house | Did you lock the front door? | Yes, I locked it twice.
@ word doormat | 🚪 🟫 | a small mat outside or inside the door, to clean your shoes on | Where's the spare key? | Under the doormat. Not very safe, I know!
@ word stairs | om:E202 | steps that go from one floor to another | Where's the bathroom? | Upstairs, go up the stairs and turn left.
@ word sofa | 🛋️ | a long, soft seat for two or more people | Where's the cat? | Asleep on the sofa, as usual.
@ word armchair | om:E141 | a comfortable chair with sides for your arms | Who sits in that armchair? | My grandfather. It's his favourite.
@ word cushion | 🛋️ 🟥 | a soft bag of cloth that you sit on or lean on | This sofa is uncomfortable. | Here, take a cushion.
@ word rug | 🟫 | a small carpet that covers part of the floor | Is this rug new? | Yes, we bought it at the market.
@ word curtains | 🪟 🎭 | pieces of cloth that hang over a window | It's too bright in here. | Close the curtains, then.
@ word lamp | 💡 🛋️ | a light that you can move, often on a table | It's dark in this corner. | Switch on the lamp.
@ word bookshelf | 📚 | a shelf or a set of shelves for books | Where do you keep your novels? | On the bookshelf by the window.
@ word remote control | 📺 🎮 | a small device to change TV channels from your seat | Have you seen the remote control? | It's under the cushion again.
@ word picture frame | om:E142 | a border around a photo or painting on the wall | Who's in that picture frame? | My parents, on their wedding day.

## REVIEW | Review 1: name the pictures
@ review doormat, stairs, sofa, armchair, cushion, curtains, lamp, bookshelf
Say the eight words. Ready?

## WORDS | The kitchen
@ word cooker | 🔥 🍳 | a large machine for cooking, with an oven and hot rings on top | Is the cooker gas or electric? | It's gas.
@ word fridge | 🧊 🥛 | a cold cupboard that keeps food fresh | Is there any milk? | Check the fridge, top shelf.
@ word sink | 🚰 | a large bowl with taps, for washing dishes | Where do I put the dirty plates? | Just leave them in the sink.
@ word tap | 🚰 💧 | the thing you turn to get water | The tap is dripping. | I'll call a plumber.
@ word kettle | 🫖 | a pot with a spout for boiling water | Shall I put the kettle on? | Yes, let's have some tea.
@ word microwave | ♨️ 📦 | a small oven that heats food very quickly | How do I warm up the soup? | Two minutes in the microwave.
@ word cupboard | 🗄️ | a piece of furniture with doors, for keeping things | Where are the glasses? | In the cupboard above the sink.
@ word drawer | 🗃️ | a box that slides in and out of furniture | Where do you keep the spoons? | In the top drawer.
@ word chopping board | 🔪 🟫 | a flat board you cut food on | Can you pass me the chopping board? | Here, the wooden one?
@ word frying pan | 🍳 | a flat pan with a long handle, for frying | Which pan should I use? | The big frying pan.

## REVIEW | Review 2: name the pictures

## WORDS | The bedroom
@ word bed | 🛏️ | the furniture you sleep on | Is this bed comfortable? | Very! I slept like a baby.
@ word pillow | 🛏️ ☁️ | a soft cushion for your head in bed | I can't sleep with one pillow. | Here, take another one.
@ word blanket | om:E31A | a thick cover that keeps you warm in bed | It's cold tonight. | There's an extra blanket in the wardrobe.
@ word wardrobe | 🚪 👗 | a tall cupboard for hanging clothes | Where do I hang my coat? | In the wardrobe in the guest room.
@ word chest of drawers | 🗃️ 🗄️ | a piece of furniture with several drawers for clothes | Where are your socks? | In the chest of drawers, bottom drawer.

@ word bedside table | 🛏️ 🕯️ | a small table next to a bed | Where are my glasses? | On your bedside table.
@ word hanger | 👔 🪝 | a shaped piece of plastic, wood or wire for hanging clothes | Can I borrow a hanger? | Sure, take as many as you need.

## WORDS | The bathroom and the rest of the house
@ word toilet | 🚽 | the seat you use to go to the toilet | Excuse me, where's the toilet? | Second door on the right.
@ word shower | 🚿 | water that sprays from above, to wash your body | How long do your showers take? | About five minutes.
@ word bathtub | 🛁 | a long container you fill with water and sit in to wash | Do you prefer a bath or a shower? | A bath, when I have time.
@ word washbasin | 🚰 🧼 | a small sink in the bathroom for washing hands and face | Where's the soap? | Next to the washbasin.
@ word towel rail | 🧻 | a bar on the wall for hanging towels | Where do I put my wet towel? | On the towel rail, please, not on the floor!
@ word light switch | om:E1C3 | a small button on the wall that turns a light on or off | I can't find the light switch. | It's on the left, behind the door.
@ word socket | om:E144 | a place in the wall where you plug in electric things | Where can I charge my phone? | There's a socket next to the desk.
@ word balcony | 🏢 🌇 | a small area outside an upstairs window or door | Where do you dry your clothes? | On the balcony, in the sun.
@ word garage | 🚗 🏠 | a building or room where you keep a car | Where's the bicycle? | In the garage, behind the car.
@ word attic | 🏠 📦 | the room or space just under the roof | Where are the old photos? | In a box up in the attic.
