# 30 Phone and Internet Words with Pictures

## INTRO
Welcome to Everyday English with Pictures. You probably use your phone every day, maybe to watch this video right now! Today you will learn thirty phone and internet words.
Guess each picture, listen, and repeat. These are words you can use today.

## WORDS | Your phone
@ word smartphone | 📱 | a mobile phone that can use the internet and apps | Is that a new smartphone? | Yes, the camera is amazing.
@ word charger | 🔌 📱 | the cable and plug you use to fill the battery | Can I borrow your charger? | Sure, it's next to the bed.
@ word battery | 🔋 | the part that stores power in a phone | Why didn't you answer? | Sorry, my battery died.
@ word screen | 📱 ✨ | the glass part of a phone that shows pictures and words | What happened to your screen? | I dropped my phone. It cracked.
@ word ringtone | 📱 🎵 | the sound your phone makes when someone calls | Is that your ringtone? | Yes, it's my favourite song.
@ word silent mode | 📱 🔕 | when your phone makes no sound | Please put your phone on silent mode. | Done. Sorry!
@ word app | 📲 | a program you download onto your phone | Which app do you use for maps? | The one that came with my phone.
@ word notification | 🔔 📱 | a message on your screen that tells you something new | Why does your phone keep beeping? | Too many notifications. I'll turn them off.
@ word screenshot | 📱 📸 | a picture of what is on your screen | How did you save the ticket? | I took a screenshot.
@ word selfie | 🤳 | a photo you take of yourself | Let's take a selfie! | OK, everyone smile!

## WORDS | Using the internet
@ word Wi-Fi | 🛜 | a way to connect to the internet without wires | What's the Wi-Fi password? | It's on the back of the router.
@ word password | 🔑 💻 | a secret word you use to enter an account | I forgot my password. | Click "forgot password" and reset it.
@ word log in | 🔓 💻 | to enter your account with your name and password | I can't log in. | Check your password, maybe caps lock is on.
@ word log out | 🔒 🚪 | to leave your account | Should I log out on this computer? | Yes, it's a shared computer.
@ word download | ⬇️ 💻 | to copy something from the internet onto your device | How do I get the app? | Download it from the app store.
@ word upload | ⬆️ ☁️ | to send something from your device to the internet | Did you upload the photos? | Yes, they're in the shared folder.
@ word search | 🔍 | to look for information on the internet | How do I find the opening times? | Search for the shop name.
@ word scroll | 📱 👆 | to move up or down a page on a screen | Where's the contact number? | Scroll down to the bottom of the page.
@ word swipe | 👉 📱 | to move your finger across a screen | How do I see the next photo? | Swipe left.
@ word tap | 👆 📱 | to touch the screen quickly with your finger | How do I open the message? | Just tap on it.

## WORDS | Calls and messages
@ word video call | 📹 📞 | a phone call where you can see each other | How do you talk to your parents abroad? | We have a video call every Sunday.
@ word mute | 🔇 | to turn off your microphone or the sound | Can you hear the dog barking? | Sorry, I'll mute myself.
@ word voice message | 🎙️ 💬 | a short recording you send instead of typing | Did you get my voice message? | Yes, I'll call you back later.
@ word text | 💬 📱 | to send a written message on a phone | Text me when you arrive. | I will, don't worry.
@ word missed call | 📞 ❌ | a call you did not answer | I have three missed calls from you! | Yes, I needed the house key.
@ word call back | 📞 🔁 | to phone someone who called you earlier | Can I call you back? I'm in a meeting. | Sure, no problem.
@ word hang up | 📞 ⬇️ | to end a phone call | Did she hang up? | Yes, the signal was bad.
@ word signal | 📶 | the connection your phone gets from the network | Why can't I call? | There's no signal in the basement.
@ word update | 🔄 📱 | to put the newest version of an app or system on your device | My phone is very slow. | Try updating it.
@ word back up | 💾 ☁️ | to save a copy of your files somewhere safe | Did you lose your photos? | No, I backed them up.
