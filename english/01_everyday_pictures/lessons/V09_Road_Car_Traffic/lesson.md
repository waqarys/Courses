# 40 Road, Car and Traffic Words with Pictures

## INTRO
Welcome to Everyday English with Pictures. Today we are on the road! You will learn forty words about roads, cars and traffic: words you need when you drive, ride, walk, or give directions.
Guess each picture, then listen and repeat. Fasten your seat belt. Let's go!

## WORDS | On the road
@ word road | 🛣️ | a wide path for cars and other vehicles | Is this the road to the station? | Yes, keep going straight.
@ word motorway | 🛣️ 🚗 | a big, fast road between cities; Americans say highway | How long does it take on the motorway? | About two hours.
@ word lane | 🛣️ ↔️ | one part of a road, for one line of cars | Why are you changing lanes? | This lane turns left.
@ word traffic lights | 🚦 | red, yellow and green lights that control traffic | Turn right at the traffic lights. | The second or the first lights?
@ word roundabout | 🔄 🚗 | a round junction where cars go around a circle | Which exit do I take at the roundabout? | The third exit.
@ word crossroads | ✝️ 🛣️ | a place where two roads cross each other | Where's the bakery? | At the crossroads, on the corner.
@ word zebra crossing | 🦓 🚶 | black and white stripes on the road where people can cross | Where should we cross? | Use the zebra crossing. It's safer.
@ word pavement | 🚶 🧱 | the path at the side of the road for people walking; Americans say sidewalk | Why are you walking in the road? | The pavement is closed for repairs.
@ word bridge | 🌉 | a structure that goes over a river or road | How do we get across the river? | There's a bridge two kilometres away.
@ word tunnel | 🚇 ⛰️ | a long passage under the ground or through a mountain | Why did the radio stop? | We're in a tunnel.

## WORDS | Traffic
@ word traffic jam | 🚗 🚙 🚕 | a long line of cars that can't move | Why are you late? | I was stuck in a traffic jam.
@ word rush hour | 🕗 🚗 | the busy time when people travel to and from work | When is the traffic worst? | At rush hour, around eight and six.
@ word speed limit | 🚫 🔢 | the fastest speed you are allowed to drive | What's the speed limit here? | Fifty kilometres an hour.
@ word speed camera | 📷 🚗 | a camera that takes a photo of cars that drive too fast | Why did you slow down? | There's a speed camera ahead.
@ word speed bump | 〰️ 🛣️ | a small raised part of the road to make cars go slowly | Why is the road bumpy? | There are speed bumps near the school.
@ word traffic police | 👮 🚦 | police who control traffic and check drivers | Who stopped you? | The traffic police. My light was broken.
@ word fine | 🧾 💸 | money you must pay because you broke a rule | Did you get a fine for parking there? | Yes, fifty dollars!
@ word accident | 💥 🚗 | when a car hits something and people may get hurt | Is the road closed? | Yes, there's been an accident.
@ word detour | ↪️ 🚧 | a different, longer route because the usual road is closed | The bridge is closed. | We'll have to take a detour.
@ word roadworks | 🚧 👷 | repairs being done to a road | Why is there so much traffic? | There are roadworks on Main Street.

## REVIEW | Review 2: name the pictures

Eight more. Say them out loud.

## WORDS | Parts of a car
@ word steering wheel | 🚗 ☸️ | the wheel you turn to drive a car | Keep both hands on the steering wheel. | Ten and two, right?
@ word seat belt | 💺 🔒 | a belt that keeps you safe in your seat | Is everyone wearing their seat belt? | Yes, we're ready.
@ word horn | 📯 🚗 | the part you press to make a loud warning sound | Why did that driver use his horn? | We were too slow at the green light.
@ word headlights | 🔦 🚗 | the big lights at the front of a car | It's getting dark. | Turn on the headlights.
@ word indicator | ⬅️ 💡 | a flashing light that shows you are turning | That car didn't use its indicator! | That's very dangerous.
@ word windscreen | 🚗 🪟 | the big front window of a car; Americans say windshield | I can't see anything! | The windscreen is dirty. Use the wipers.
@ word wipers | 🌧️ 🚗 | arms that clean rain from the windscreen | It's raining hard. | Put the wipers on fast.
@ word boot | 🚗 📦 | the space at the back of a car for luggage; Americans say trunk | Where can I put my suitcase? | In the boot, there's lots of space.
@ word tyre | 🛞 | the rubber ring around a wheel | The tyres look old. | I'm getting new ones next week.
@ word brakes | 🛑 🦶 | the part that makes a car slow down or stop | Why did the car stop so suddenly? | I had to hit the brakes. A dog ran into the road.

## REVIEW | Review 3: name the pictures

Third review. Eight pictures.

## WORDS | Driving and parking
@ word driving licence | 🪪 🚗 | an official card that shows you can drive | Can I see your driving licence? | Here it is.
@ word petrol station | ⛽ | a place where you buy fuel for your car; Americans say gas station | We need fuel. | There's a petrol station after the bridge.
@ word fill up | ⛽ 🚗 | to fill the tank with fuel | How much does it cost to fill up? | About sixty dollars.
@ word car park | 🅿️ | an area or building where you leave cars; Americans say parking lot | Where did you park? | In the car park behind the mall.
@ word parking space | 🅿️ 🚙 | a place for one car | I can't find a parking space. | Try the next street.
@ word flat tyre | 🛞 😟 | a tyre with no air in it | Why have you stopped? | I think we have a flat tyre.
@ word break down | 🚗 💨 | when a car stops working | Why are you so late? | My car broke down on the motorway.
@ word overtake | 🚗 ➡️ 🚙 | to go past a slower car | Is it safe to overtake here? | No, wait until the road is straight.
@ word reverse | ⬅️ 🚗 | to drive backwards | Can you reverse into that space? | I'll try!
@ word give someone a lift | 🚗 👍 | to take someone somewhere in your car | Can you give me a lift to the station? | Sure, hop in.
