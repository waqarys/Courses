# From Morning to Night: 50 Daily Routine Verbs with Pictures

## INTRO
Welcome to Everyday English with Pictures. What do you do every day? Today you will learn fifty verbs and phrases to describe your daily routine, from the moment you wake up to the moment you fall asleep.
Guess each picture, then listen and repeat. You will hear each phrase in a real question and answer. At the end, try to describe your own day in English.

## WORDS | Early morning
@ word wake up | ⏰ 😴 | to stop sleeping | What time do you usually wake up? | Around six thirty on weekdays.
@ word snooze the alarm | ⏰ 💤 | to press a button so the alarm rings again a few minutes later | Why are you late? | I snoozed the alarm three times.
@ word get up | 🛏️ 🧍 | to get out of bed | Do you get up as soon as you wake up? | No, I stay in bed for five more minutes.
@ word stretch | 🙆 | to make your arms, legs and back long to wake up your body | What's the first thing you do in the morning? | I stretch for a minute.
@ word go to the toilet | 🚽 | to use the toilet | Can you wait for me? | Sure, go to the toilet first.
@ word wash your face | 🧼 💧 | to clean your face with water and soap | How do you wake yourself up? | I wash my face with cold water.
@ word brush your teeth | 🪥 | to clean your teeth with a toothbrush | How often do you brush your teeth? | Twice a day, morning and night.
@ word take a shower | 🚿 | to wash your body under a shower | Do you shower in the morning or at night? | In the morning. It wakes me up.
@ word shave | 🪒 | to cut hair from your face or body with a razor | You look different today. | I shaved off my beard.
@ word comb your hair | 💇 | to make your hair tidy with a comb | Your hair is a mess. | Oops, I forgot to comb it.
