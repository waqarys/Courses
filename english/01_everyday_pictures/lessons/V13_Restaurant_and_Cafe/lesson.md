# 30 Restaurant and Café Words with Pictures

## INTRO
Welcome to Everyday English with Pictures. Are you hungry? Today we eat out! You will learn thirty words you need in a restaurant or a café, from booking a table to paying the bill.
Guess each picture, then listen and repeat. Every word comes with a real question and answer you can use next time you order.

## WORDS | Arriving and ordering
@ word book a table | 📞 🍽️ | to reserve a table in a restaurant | Did you book a table? | Yes, for four people at eight.
@ word menu | 📜 🍽️ | the list of food and drinks and their prices | Could we see the menu, please? | Of course, here you are.
@ word waiter | 🤵 🍽️ | a person who brings food to your table | Excuse me, are you our waiter? | Yes, I'll be right with you.
@ word order | 📝 🍔 | to ask for food or drink in a restaurant | Are you ready to order? | Yes, I'll have the fish curry.
@ word starter | 🥗 | a small dish you eat before the main meal | Would you like a starter? | Yes, the tomato soup, please.
@ word main course | 🍛 | the biggest part of the meal | What's your favourite main course here? | The grilled chicken.
@ word side dish | 🍟 | a small extra dish served with the main course | Does it come with a side dish? | Yes, rice or chips.
@ word dessert | 🍰 | something sweet you eat at the end of a meal | Any room for dessert? | Always! What do you have?
@ word vegetarian | 🥦 🚫 🍗 | food with no meat or fish | Do you have vegetarian dishes? | Yes, they have a green leaf on the menu.
@ word allergy | ⚠️ 🥜 | when your body reacts badly to a food | Do you have any allergies? | Yes, I'm allergic to nuts.

## WORDS | At the table
@ word napkin | 🧻 🍽️ | a piece of cloth or paper for cleaning your mouth and hands | Could I have another napkin? | Sure, here you go.
@ word cutlery | 🍴 | knives, forks and spoons | There's no cutlery on our table. | Sorry, I'll bring some now.
@ word jug of water | 🫗 💧 | a container of water for the table | Could we have a jug of water? | Of course, tap water is free.
@ word spicy | 🌶️ | with a strong, hot taste | Is this dish spicy? | Very! Shall I make it mild?
@ word delicious | 😋 | tasting very good | How's the pasta? | Delicious, thank you.
@ word refill | 🔄 ☕ | to fill a cup or glass again | Can I get a refill? | Yes, refills are free.
@ word highchair | 🪑 👶 | a tall chair for a baby to sit at the table | Do you have a highchair? | Yes, I'll bring one over.
@ word leftovers | 🥡 🍛 | food that is not finished at the end of a meal | Can I take the leftovers home? | Of course, I'll put them in a box.
@ word takeaway | 🥡 | food you buy at a restaurant and eat at home | Shall we cook tonight? | No, let's get a takeaway.
@ word delivery | 🛵 🍕 | when food is brought to your home | How long is the delivery? | About thirty minutes.

## WORDS | At the café and paying
@ word café | ☕ 🥐 | a small place that sells drinks and light food | Shall we meet at the café? | Good idea, the one near the park.
@ word barista | om:E185 | a person who makes coffee in a café | What's your name for the cup? | It's Sam. Thanks!
@ word latte | om:E0C5 | a coffee made with a lot of hot milk | What would you like? | A large latte, please.
@ word for here or to go | 🏠 ↔️ 🥤 | a question: will you eat in the café, or take it with you? | For here or to go? | To go, please.
@ word pastry | 🥐 | a small sweet or savoury baked food | Anything to eat? | Just a pastry, please.
@ word counter | 🏪 ☕ | the long table where you order and pay | Where do I order? | At the counter, then sit anywhere.
@ word bill | 🧾 | the paper that shows how much you must pay; Americans say check | Could we have the bill, please? | Certainly, I'll bring it right away.
@ word tip | 💵 👍 | extra money you give the waiter for good service | Is service included? | No, but you can leave a tip.
@ word split the bill | 🧾 ✂️ | to share the cost of a meal | Shall we split the bill? | Yes, let's pay half each.
@ word keep the change | 🪙 😊 | words you say when the waiter can keep the extra money | Here's your change. | That's fine, keep the change.
