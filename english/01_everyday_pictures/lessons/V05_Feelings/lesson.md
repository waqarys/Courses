# 40 Feelings and Emotions with Pictures

## INTRO
Welcome to Everyday English with Pictures. When someone asks how are you, most people say: fine, thanks. But English has many more words for how we feel.
Today you will learn forty feelings and emotions. Look at each face and guess the word. Then listen, repeat, and hear it in a real conversation.

## WORDS | Good feelings
@ word happy | 😀 | feeling good and pleased | You look happy today! | I am. I got the job!
@ word excited | 🤩 | very happy about something that will happen soon | Are you excited about your trip? | Yes, I can't wait!
@ word relaxed | 😌 | calm and not worried | How was your holiday? | Lovely. I feel so relaxed now.
@ word proud | 😊 🏅 | pleased because you or someone close to you did something well | How do you feel about your exam result? | Really proud. I studied so hard.
@ word grateful | 🙏 | wanting to thank someone for something they did | Thank you for your help. | I'm really grateful.
@ word relieved | 😮‍💨 | happy because something bad didn't happen, or is finished | The test was cancelled! | Oh, I'm so relieved.
@ word hopeful | 🌈 🙂 | believing something good will happen | Do you think you got the job? | I'm hopeful. The interview went well.
@ word confident | 😎 | sure that you can do something well | Are you nervous about the presentation? | No, I'm confident. I practised a lot.
@ word amused | 😄 | finding something funny | Why are you smiling? | I'm amused by this cat video.
@ word in love | 😍 | having strong romantic feelings for someone | How did they meet? | At work. They're very much in love.

## REVIEW | Review 1: name the faces
@ review happy, excited, relaxed, proud, grateful, relieved, confident, in love
How do they feel? Say each word.
Six or more? Great! Subscribe for a new picture lesson every week.

## WORDS | Bad feelings
@ word sad | 😢 | unhappy | Why are you sad? | My best friend is moving to another city.
@ word upset | 😟 | unhappy and worried because something bad happened | Is she upset? | A little. Someone was rude to her at work.
@ word angry | 😠 | having a strong feeling against someone who did something bad | Why is the manager so angry? | The delivery is late again.
@ word furious | 😡 | extremely angry | Was your dad angry about the car? | Angry? He was furious!
@ word annoyed | 😒 | a little angry | Are you annoyed with me? | A bit. You didn't call back.
@ word disappointed | 😞 | sad because something was not as good as you hoped | How was the film? | Disappointing. I expected more.
@ word lonely | 😔 🪑 | sad because you are alone | Do you ever feel lonely in the new city? | Sometimes, in the evenings.
@ word jealous | 😒 💚 | unhappy because someone has something you want | Are you jealous of her new car? | Maybe just a little!
@ word guilty | 😣 | feeling bad because you did something wrong | Why do you feel guilty? | I ate the last piece of cake.
@ word heartbroken | 💔 | extremely sad, often because love ended | How is he after the break-up? | Heartbroken, but he'll be OK.

## REVIEW | Review 2: name the faces
@ review sad, angry, furious, annoyed, disappointed, lonely, jealous, heartbroken
Eight more feelings. Go!

## WORDS | Worry and fear
@ word nervous | 😬 | a little afraid and worried about something | Are you nervous about the interview? | Yes, my hands are shaking.
@ word worried | 😟 💭 | thinking a lot about a problem or something bad that might happen | You look worried. | My flight might be cancelled.
@ word anxious | 😰 | very worried and uneasy | Why can't you sleep? | I'm anxious about tomorrow.
@ word scared | 😨 | afraid | Are you scared of spiders? | Terrified! Please take it away.
@ word terrified | 😱 | extremely scared | How was the horror film? | I was terrified the whole time.
@ word stressed | 😫 | feeling worried because of too much work or too many problems | You seem stressed. | I have three deadlines this week.
@ word shocked | 😲 | very surprised by something bad | Did you hear the news? | Yes, I was shocked.
@ word surprised | 😮 | feeling something unexpected happened | Were you surprised by the party? | Totally! I had no idea.
@ word embarrassed | 😳 | feeling silly because people saw you do something wrong | Why is your face red? | I called my teacher Mum. So embarrassing!
@ word confused | 😕 | not able to understand something | Are you confused? | Yes. Can you explain it again?

## REVIEW | Review 3: name the faces
@ review nervous, worried, scared, stressed, shocked, surprised, embarrassed, confused
Eight more. How do they feel?

## WORDS | How your body feels
@ word tired | 🥱 | needing rest or sleep | You look tired. | I worked late last night.
@ word exhausted | 😩 | extremely tired | How was the marathon? | Amazing, but I'm exhausted.
@ word sleepy | 😪 | wanting to sleep | Do you want more coffee? | Yes, I'm so sleepy.
@ word bored | 😑 | not interested, with nothing to do | Are you bored? | A little. This meeting is very long.
@ word hungry | 😋 🍽️ | wanting to eat | Are you hungry? | Starving! Let's order pizza.
@ word thirsty | 🥤 😮 | wanting to drink | Are you thirsty? | Yes, can I have some water?
@ word calm | 🧘 | quiet and peaceful, not worried | How do you stay so calm? | I breathe slowly and count to ten.
@ word sick | 🤢 | feeling ill, as if you might vomit | Why didn't you come to work? | I felt sick all morning.
@ word homesick | 🏠 😢 | sad because you are far from home and family | How's life abroad? | Good, but I'm a bit homesick.
@ word fed up | 😤 | bored or annoyed with something that has continued too long | Why are you leaving your job? | I'm fed up with the long hours.

## REVIEW | Review 4: name the faces
@ review tired, exhausted, sleepy, bored, hungry, calm, sick, homesick
Last review.
