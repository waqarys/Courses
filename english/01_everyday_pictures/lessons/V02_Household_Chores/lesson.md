# 50 Household Chores with Pictures

## INTRO
Welcome back to Everyday English with Pictures. Nobody loves housework, but everybody talks about it! Today you will learn fifty household chores.
A chore is a small job we do regularly to keep the home clean and tidy. Look at each picture and guess first. Then listen, repeat, and learn how to use it in a question and an answer.
There are five quick reviews and a final test. Keep your score!

## WORDS | Floors and surfaces
We start with the floor and the furniture.
@ word sweep the floor | 🧹 | to clean the floor with a broom | Who swept the kitchen floor? | I did, there were crumbs everywhere.
@ word mop the floor | 🪣 🧹 | to wash the floor with a wet mop | Can I walk in the hall? | Wait, I've just mopped the floor. It's still wet.
@ word vacuum the carpet | 🧹 om:E144 | to clean a carpet with a vacuum cleaner, a machine that sucks up dirt | When do you vacuum? | Every Saturday morning.
@ word dust the shelves | 🪶 📚 | to remove dust with a soft cloth or a duster | Why are you sneezing? | I was dusting the shelves.
@ word polish | ✨ 🪑 | to rub something until it shines | The table looks great! | Thanks, I polished it this morning.
@ word wipe the counter | 🧽 om:E0AE | to clean the kitchen worktop with a cloth | Can you wipe the counter after you cook? | Of course, I always do.
@ word clean the windows | 🪟 🧽 | to wash the glass so you can see through it clearly | The windows are so dirty. | I'll clean them this weekend.
@ word disinfect | om:E0B1 | to kill germs with a special spray or liquid | Why do you disinfect the door handles? | Because everybody touches them.
@ word empty the bin | 🗑️ | to take everything out of the rubbish bin | The bin is full again. | Don't worry, I'll empty it.
@ word recycle | ♻️ | to put paper, glass and plastic in a special bin, so they can be used again | Do you recycle bottles? | Yes, we have a blue bin for them.

## REVIEW | Review 1: name the pictures
@ review sweep the floor, mop the floor, dust the shelves, polish, clean the windows, disinfect, empty the bin, recycle
Say the eight chores before the timer ends.
Six or more? Great work! If this is helping you, subscribe, because a new picture lesson comes every week.

## WORDS | Laundry day
Now, the laundry. Laundry means clothes that need washing, or clothes that are just washed.
@ word do the laundry | 🧺 | to wash dirty clothes, sheets and towels | What are you doing on Sunday? | Just doing the laundry and relaxing.
@ word sort the clothes | 👕 👖 | to put clothes into groups, for example whites and colours | Why do you sort the clothes? | So the colours don't run onto the white shirts.
@ word load the washing machine | om:E1CF | to put dirty clothes into the washing machine | Did you load the washing machine? | Yes, it's running now.
@ word hang up the washing | 👚 ☀️ | to put wet clothes on a line or rack to dry | It's sunny today! | Great, let's hang up the washing outside.
@ word take in the washing | 🧺 👚 | to bring dry clothes back inside | It's starting to rain! | Quick, take in the washing!
@ word fold the clothes | 👕 | to bend clothes neatly into a smaller shape | Can you help me fold the clothes? | Sure, I'll do the towels.
@ word iron a shirt | 👔 ♨️ | to make clothes smooth with a hot iron | Your shirt looks very smart. | Thanks, I ironed it last night.
@ word hang up | 🧥 | to put clothes on a hanger or a hook | Where's my jacket? | I hung it up in the wardrobe.
@ word put away the clothes | 🗄️ 👕 | to put clean clothes back in drawers and cupboards | Are these clean? | Yes, please put them away.
@ word sew on a button | 🪡 🧵 | to fix a button onto clothes with a needle and thread | My button came off. | Give it to me, I'll sew it on.

## REVIEW | Review 2: name the pictures
@ review do the laundry, sort the clothes, hang up the washing, take in the washing, fold the clothes, iron a shirt, hang up, sew on a button
Eight more. Say each chore aloud.

## WORDS | Bedroom and bathroom
Let's go upstairs to the bedroom and bathroom.
@ word make the bed | 🛏️ | to tidy the sheets and pillows after you get up | Do you make your bed every morning? | Yes, it takes one minute.
@ word change the sheets | om:E31A | to take off old sheets and put on clean ones | When did you last change the sheets? | Last Sunday. I change them every week.
@ word air the room | 🪟 💨 | to open the windows to let fresh air in | It smells a bit stuffy in here. | Let's open the window and air the room.
@ word tidy up | 🧸 📦 | to put things back in the right place | Your room is a mess! | I know, I'll tidy up after lunch.
@ word clean the toilet | 🚽 🧽 | to wash the toilet with a brush and cleaner | Whose turn is it to clean the toilet? | Mine, unfortunately.
@ word scrub the bathtub | 🛁 🧽 | to rub the bath hard to remove dirt | Why is the bathtub so shiny? | I scrubbed it this morning.
@ word clean the mirror | 🪞 | to wipe the glass so it has no marks | I can't see myself clearly. | The mirror needs cleaning.
@ word replace the towels | 🧻 🔄 | to put fresh, clean towels in place of used ones | The towels are damp. | I'll replace them with clean ones.
@ word unclog the drain | 🚿 🪠 | to clear something that is blocking the drain | The water isn't going down. | We need to unclog the drain.
@ word refill the soap | 🧴 | to fill the soap bottle again | We're out of hand soap. | There's a refill under the sink.

## REVIEW | Review 3: name the pictures
@ review make the bed, change the sheets, air the room, tidy up, clean the toilet, scrub the bathtub, clean the mirror, unclog the drain
Third review. Eight pictures, eight chores.

## WORDS | Kitchen chores
Now the kitchen, the busiest room in the house.
@ word do the dishes | 🍽️ 🫧 | to wash plates, cups and pans after a meal | I cooked, so you do the dishes. | That's fair.
@ word wash up | 🫧 🍽️ | another way to say do the dishes, common in British English | Shall I wash up? | Yes please, I'll dry.
@ word clear the table | 🍽️ ➡️ | to take plates and food off the table after eating | Can you help me clear the table? | Sure, I'll take the glasses.
@ word clean the fridge | 🧊 🧽 | to take everything out of the fridge and wipe it inside | Something smells bad in the fridge. | It's time to clean it.
@ word defrost the freezer | ❄️ 💧 | to let the ice in the freezer melt | Why is there water on the floor? | I'm defrosting the freezer.
@ word sweep up the crumbs | 🍞 🧹 | to collect small bits of food with a brush | There are crumbs under the table. | I'll sweep them up.
@ word take out the recycling | ♻️ 🚪 | to carry the recycling bin outside for collection | When do they collect recycling? | On Tuesday mornings.
@ word buy groceries | 🛒 🥕 | to buy food and things for the home | We need milk and eggs. | I'll buy groceries on my way home.
@ word put away the groceries | 🛍️ 🗄️ | to put the shopping in the fridge and cupboards | Where do the eggs go? | In the fridge, on the top shelf.
@ word plan the meals | 📝 🍲 | to decide what to cook for the next few days | What's for dinner this week? | I'll plan the meals tonight.

## REVIEW | Review 4: name the pictures

Fourth review. You are doing great.

## WORDS | Outside and other jobs
Finally, jobs outside the house, and some jobs that don't fit anywhere else.
@ word water the plants | 🪴 💧 | to give plants water | Can you water the plants while I'm away? | Of course, every two days?
@ word mow the lawn | om:E343 | to cut the grass with a lawnmower | The grass is really long. | I'll mow the lawn on Sunday.
@ word rake the leaves | 🍂 | to collect fallen leaves with a rake | Why are you in the garden? | I'm raking the leaves.
@ word wash the car | 🚗 🫧 | to clean a car with water and soap | Your car looks brand new! | I washed it this morning.
@ word feed the cat | 🐈 🥣 | to give an animal its food | Did anyone feed the cat? | Yes, she's had her breakfast.
@ word walk the dog | 🐕 🦮 | to take a dog outside for exercise | Who's walking the dog tonight? | It's my turn.
@ word fix | 🔧 | to repair something that is broken | The tap is dripping again. | I'll fix it this evening.
@ word change a light bulb | 💡 | to take out an old bulb and put in a new one | The kitchen light isn't working. | It just needs a new bulb.
@ word pay the bills | 🧾 💳 | to pay money for electricity, water, internet and so on | Did you pay the electricity bill? | Yes, I paid it online yesterday.
@ word declutter | 📦 ✨ | to remove things you don't need, to make space | Your room looks bigger! | I decluttered. I gave away three bags of clothes.
