# 40 Travel and Airport Words with Pictures

## INTRO
Welcome to Everyday English with Pictures. Pack your bags: today we are going to the airport! You will learn forty words for travel, from booking a ticket to collecting your suitcase.
Guess each picture, listen, and repeat. Every word comes with a question and answer you might really hear on your trip.

## WORDS | Before you fly
@ word book a ticket | 🎫 💻 | to buy or reserve a ticket in advance | Did you book the tickets? | Yes, I booked them online last week.
@ word passport | 🛂 📘 | an official document you need to travel to another country | Can I see your passport, please? | Here you are.
@ word visa | 📄 🛂 | official permission to enter a country | Do I need a visa for Japan? | It depends on your nationality.
@ word suitcase | 🧳 | a large case for carrying clothes when you travel | Is this suitcase yours? | Yes, the blue one.
@ word pack | 🧳 👕 | to put things in a bag or suitcase for a trip | Have you packed yet? | Not yet. I'll do it tonight.
@ word luggage | 🧳 🎒 | all the bags and suitcases you take on a trip | How much luggage do you have? | Just one suitcase and a backpack.
@ word hand luggage | 🎒 | a small bag you can take onto the plane with you | Can I take this bag on the plane? | Yes, it's fine as hand luggage.
@ word travel insurance | 📄 ☂️ | a plan that pays if something goes wrong on your trip | Did you buy travel insurance? | Yes, just in case.
@ word itinerary | 🗺️ 📋 | a plan of your journey, with places and times | What's on the itinerary for tomorrow? | A city tour in the morning, then the museum.
@ word taxi | 🚕 | a car you pay to take you somewhere | How are you getting to the airport? | I've booked a taxi for six.

@ review book a ticket, passport, visa, suitcase, pack, hand luggage, itinerary, taxi
Say the eight words.
Six or more? You are ready to travel! Subscribe for a new picture lesson every week.

## WORDS | At the airport
@ word terminal | 🛫 🏢 | a large building at an airport where passengers arrive and leave | Which terminal is your flight from? | Terminal two.
@ word departures board | 🖥️ 🛫 | a screen that shows flights leaving and their gates | Is our flight on time? | Let's look at the departures board.
@ word check-in desk | 🧑‍💼 🧳 | the place where you show your ticket and leave your big bags | Where's the check-in desk for Air India? | Row C, on the right.
@ word boarding pass | 🎫 ✈️ | the card that lets you get on the plane | Do you have your boarding pass? | Yes, it's on my phone.
@ word check in a bag | 🧳 🏷️ | to give your suitcase to the airline so it travels in the hold | Are you checking in any bags? | Just one, please.
@ word excess baggage | 🧳 ⚖️ | luggage that weighs more than you are allowed | Your bag is three kilos over. | Do I have to pay for excess baggage?
@ word security check | 🛂 🔍 | the place where they check your bags and your body for dangerous things | Why is the line so long? | It's for the security check.
@ word passport control | 🛂 | the place where officers check your passport | How long did passport control take? | Only ten minutes.
@ word duty-free shop | 🛍️ ✈️ | a shop at an airport where you don't pay some taxes | Did you buy anything? | Just some chocolate at the duty-free shop.
@ word gate | 🚪 ✈️ | the door where you get on the plane | Which gate are we at? | Gate twenty-three. We should hurry.

## REVIEW | Review 2: name the pictures
@ review terminal, departures board, check-in desk, boarding pass, excess baggage, security check, duty-free shop, gate
Eight more. Say them out loud.

## WORDS | On the plane
@ word board | 🚶 ✈️ | to get on a plane, train or ship | When do we board? | In about twenty minutes.
@ word aisle seat | 💺 ↔️ | a seat next to the path between the rows | Window or aisle seat? | Aisle, please. I like to stretch my legs.
@ word window seat | 💺 🪟 | a seat next to the window | Can we change seats? | Sure. Do you want the window seat?
@ word overhead locker | 🧳 ⬆️ | the space above the seats for hand luggage | Where can I put my bag? | In the overhead locker.
@ word seat belt | 💺 🔒 | a belt that holds you in your seat | Please fasten your seat belt. | Oh, sorry, I forgot.
@ word flight attendant | 🧑‍✈️ | a person who looks after passengers on a plane | Who should I ask for a blanket? | Ask the flight attendant.
@ word take off | 🛫 | when a plane leaves the ground | When does the plane take off? | At ten fifteen.
@ word turbulence | ✈️ 〰️ | sudden, rough movement of a plane in the air | Why is the plane shaking? | It's just a little turbulence.
@ word land | 🛬 | when a plane comes down onto the ground | What time do we land? | Around two in the afternoon, local time.
@ word jet lag | 😴 🌍 | feeling tired after flying across time zones | How do you feel after the long flight? | I have terrible jet lag.

## REVIEW | Review 3: name the pictures
@ review board, aisle seat, window seat, overhead locker, seat belt, take off, turbulence, land
Third review. Eight pictures.

## WORDS | After you land
@ word arrivals | 🛬 🏢 | the part of the airport where passengers come in | Where should I meet you? | At arrivals, near the coffee shop.
@ word baggage claim | 🧳 🔄 | the place where you collect your suitcases | Where do I get my suitcase? | At baggage claim, belt number four.
@ word lost luggage | 🧳 ❓ | bags that the airline cannot find | My suitcase didn't arrive! | Go to the lost luggage desk.
@ word customs | 🛃 | the place where officers check what you bring into a country | Do you have anything to declare at customs? | No, nothing.
@ word currency exchange | 💱 | a place where you change money into another currency | Where can I change money? | There's a currency exchange in arrivals.
@ word delay | ⏱️ 😟 | when something happens later than planned | Why is the flight late? | There's a two-hour delay because of the weather.
@ word cancel | ❌ ✈️ | to say that something will not happen | Was your flight cancelled? | Yes, because of the storm.
@ word connecting flight | ✈️ 🔁 | a second flight you take to reach your final place | Is this a direct flight? | No, I have a connecting flight in Dubai.
@ word round trip | 🔁 🎫 | a ticket to go somewhere and come back | One way or round trip? | Round trip, please.
@ word souvenir | 🎁 🗽 | something you buy to remember a place | What did you bring back? | A small souvenir for everyone at work.
