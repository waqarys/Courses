# 40 Sound Words in English with Pictures

## INTRO
Welcome to Everyday English with Pictures. Listen. English has special words for sounds. A bee buzzes. A door creaks. Oil sizzles in the pan.
Today you will learn forty sound words. Many of them sound like the noise they describe, which makes them fun to say. Guess the picture, then listen and repeat.

## WORDS | Sounds at home
@ word ring | 📱 🔔 | the sound a phone or a bell makes | Is that your phone ringing? | Yes, sorry, it's my mum.
@ word beep | 📟 | a short, high electronic sound | Why is the microwave beeping? | Your food is ready.
@ word tick | ⏰ | the small, regular sound of a clock | I can't sleep. | The clock is ticking so loudly.
@ word drip | 🚰 💧 | the sound of drops of water falling | What's that noise? | The tap is dripping again.
@ word creak | 🚪 | a long, low sound of old wood or a door moving | Why are you walking so slowly? | The floor creaks, and the baby is asleep.
@ word slam | 🚪 💥 | the loud noise of a door closing hard | Please don't slam the door! | Sorry, it was the wind.
@ word knock | ✊ 🚪 | the sound of hitting a door to say you are there | Did someone knock? | Yes, it's the delivery man.
@ word whistle | 🫖 💨 | a high sound, like air or steam through a small hole | Is the water ready? | Listen, the kettle is whistling.
@ word sizzle | 🍳 | the sound of food frying in hot oil | That smells great! | Listen to those onions sizzle.
@ word hum | 🧊 🎵 | a low, steady sound, like a fridge | What's that noise in the kitchen? | It's just the fridge humming.

Say the eight sound words before the timer ends.

## WORDS | Animal sounds
@ word bark | 🐕 | the sound a dog makes | Why is the dog barking? | Someone is at the gate.
@ word meow | 🐈 | the sound a cat makes | Why is the cat meowing? | She's hungry.
@ word buzz | 🐝 | the sound of a bee or a fly | What's that buzzing sound? | There's a bee in the room!
@ word chirp | 🐦 | the short, high sound of a small bird | What woke you up? | The birds chirping outside my window.
@ word moo | 🐄 | the sound a cow makes | Did you hear that? | Yes, a cow mooing. We're near a farm.
@ word roar | 🦁 | the loud, deep sound of a lion | What was that? | A lion roaring. We're near the zoo.
@ word hiss | 🐍 | a long s sound, like a snake | Is that snake dangerous? | It's hissing, so stay back.
@ word quack | 🦆 | the sound a duck makes | Where's that quacking coming from? | There are ducks by the pond.
@ word crow | 🐓 | the loud morning sound of a rooster | What time does the rooster crow? | At five! Every morning!
@ word growl | 🐕 😠 | a low, angry sound an animal makes | Why is the dog growling? | He doesn't like strangers.

## WORDS | Sounds people make
@ word snore | 😴 💤 | to breathe noisily while sleeping | Did you sleep well? | Not really. My brother snores.
@ word sneeze | 🤧 | a sudden burst of air from your nose | Bless you! | Thanks. I keep sneezing today.
@ word cough | 😷 | to push air out of your throat noisily | Are you sick? | Just a little cough.
@ word hiccup | 😮 💥 | a small sound you make suddenly and can't stop | How do you stop hiccups? | Drink some water slowly.
@ word yawn | 🥱 | to open your mouth wide because you are tired | Am I boring you? | No! I'm just yawning because it's late.
@ word sigh | 😮‍💨 | to breathe out loudly because you are tired, sad or relieved | Why did you sigh? | Another meeting. Sigh.
@ word whisper | 🤫 | to speak very quietly | Why are you whispering? | We're in the library.
@ word shout | 📢 | to speak very loudly | Why are you shouting? | Sorry, the music is so loud.
@ word giggle | 🤭 | to laugh quietly in a silly way | What are you giggling about? | Nothing. It's a funny message.
@ word clap | 👏 | to hit your hands together to show you like something | Why is everyone clapping? | The singer just came on stage.

## REVIEW | Review 3: name the sounds

Third review. Eight pictures.

## WORDS | Sounds outside
@ word honk | 🚗 📯 | the sound of a car horn | Why is everyone honking? | The traffic light is green and the bus isn't moving.
@ word rumble | ⛈️ | a long, low, deep sound, like thunder | Did you hear that? | Thunder rumbling. A storm is coming.
@ word crash | 💥 | the loud noise of something hitting something and breaking | What was that crash? | A shelf fell in the garage.
@ word bang | 🎈 💥 | a sudden loud noise, like a balloon bursting | Why did you jump? | A balloon went bang next to me.
@ word splash | 🌊 💦 | the sound of something hitting water | What was that splash? | My phone fell in the pool!
@ word rustle | 🍂 | the soft sound of leaves or paper moving | What's that rustling in the bushes? | Probably just a cat.
@ word crunch | 🍎 | the sound of biting something hard, or walking on snow | Do you like these apples? | Yes, they're so crunchy.
@ word pop | 🍿 | a short, light sound, like popcorn cooking | Is the popcorn ready? | Not yet, it's still popping.
@ word tap | 👆 🪟 | a light, gentle knock | What's that tapping on the window? | Just the rain.
@ word screech | 🚗 💨 | a loud, high, unpleasant sound, like car tyres | Did you hear that screech? | A car stopped very suddenly.
