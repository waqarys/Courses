# 40 Garden and Plant Words with Pictures

## INTRO
Welcome to Everyday English with Pictures. Today we step outside into the garden. You will learn forty words about plants, garden tools, and the jobs gardeners do.
Even if you only have one plant on your windowsill, these words are useful. Guess the picture, listen, and repeat.

## WORDS | Parts of a plant
@ word seed | 🌰 | the small thing a new plant grows from | What are you planting? | Tomato seeds, in these little pots.
@ word soil | 🟫 🌱 | the earth that plants grow in | Why isn't my plant growing? | Maybe it needs better soil.
@ word root | 🌱 ⬇️ | the part of a plant that grows under the ground | Can I move this plant? | Yes, but be careful with the roots.
@ word stem | 🌷 | the long, thin part that holds up the leaves and flowers | Why is the flower bending? | The stem is too weak.
@ word leaf | 🍃 | a flat green part of a plant | Why are the leaves yellow? | You're giving it too much water.
@ word petal | 🌸 | one of the coloured parts of a flower | What colour are the petals? | Pink, with a white centre.
@ word bud | 🌹 🌱 | a young flower before it opens | When will the roses open? | Soon. Look, there are lots of buds.
@ word thorn | 🌹 ⚠️ | a sharp point on a plant's stem | Ouch! | Careful, roses have thorns.
@ word branch | 🌳 | a part of a tree that grows out from the trunk | Can you reach the mangoes? | Only the ones on the low branch.
@ word trunk | 🌳 🪵 | the thick main part of a tree | How old is that tree? | Very old. Look how thick the trunk is.

## WORDS | Garden tools
@ word watering can | 🪴 💧 | a container with a long spout for watering plants | Where's the watering can? | Next to the back door.
@ word hose | 🚿 🌳 | a long tube that carries water | It's too far to carry water. | Use the hose.
@ word spade | om:E345 | a tool with a flat metal end for digging | Can I borrow your spade? | Sure, are you planting a tree?
@ word trowel | om:E346 | a small hand tool for digging little holes | What do I use for these small plants? | Use the trowel.
@ word rake | 🍂 🧹 | a tool with long teeth for collecting leaves or making soil flat | Where are you going with the rake? | To clear the leaves from the lawn.
@ word wheelbarrow | om:E344 | a small cart with one wheel, for carrying heavy things | How do we move all this soil? | Use the wheelbarrow.
@ word lawn mower | om:E343 | a machine that cuts grass | What's that loud noise? | Our neighbour's lawn mower.
@ word gardening gloves | om:E342 | thick gloves that protect your hands in the garden | Why are you wearing gloves? | So the thorns don't hurt me.
@ word pot | 🪴 | a container for growing plants | This plant is too big for its pot. | Let's move it to a bigger one.
@ word greenhouse | 🏠 🌱 | a glass building where plants grow | How do you grow tomatoes in winter? | In the greenhouse.

## WORDS | Plants and flowers
@ word lawn | 🌿 🏡 | an area of short grass | Can we play football on the lawn? | Yes, but not near the flowers.
@ word bush | 🌳 🌿 | a low plant with many branches | Where did the ball go? | Into the bush, over there.
@ word hedge | 🌳 🟩 | a line of bushes that grow close together | Who cuts the hedge? | My neighbour does it every month.
@ word weed | 🌿 ❌ | a wild plant growing where you don't want it | What are these plants? | Weeds. Let's pull them out.
@ word herbs | 🌿 🍃 | plants used for flavour in cooking, like mint and basil | Do you grow anything? | Just herbs on my balcony.
@ word sunflower | 🌻 | a very tall plant with a big yellow flower | How tall is your sunflower? | Taller than me!
@ word cactus | 🌵 | a plant with thick stems and sharp points that needs little water | Which plant is easy to look after? | A cactus. Water it once a month.
@ word vegetable patch | 🥕 🥬 | a small area for growing vegetables | What's in your vegetable patch? | Carrots, beans and spinach.
@ word fruit tree | 🍎 🌳 | a tree that grows fruit | Is that a fruit tree? | Yes, it's a lemon tree.
@ word compost | ♻️ 🍂 | old plants and food waste that become good soil | What do you do with vegetable peel? | We put it on the compost.

## REVIEW | Review 3: name the pictures
@ review lawn, bush, hedge, weed, herbs, sunflower, cactus, fruit tree
Third review. Eight pictures.

## WORDS | Garden jobs
@ word plant | 🌱 🤲 | to put a seed or young plant in the soil | When did you plant these? | Last spring.
@ word dig | om:E345 🟫 | to make a hole in the ground | What's the dog doing? | Digging a hole in my flowers!
@ word water | 💧 🪴 | to give a plant water | How often do you water the plants? | Every morning in summer.
@ word grow | 🌱 📈 | to get bigger | How fast does bamboo grow? | Very fast, almost a metre a day!
@ word bloom | 🌸 ✨ | when flowers open | When do the cherry trees bloom? | In early spring.
@ word wilt | 🥀 | when a plant becomes weak and bends because it needs water | Why are the flowers wilting? | It's too hot. Give them water.
@ word prune | ✂️ 🌳 | to cut off parts of a plant to help it grow well | Why are you cutting the rose bush? | I'm pruning it. It will grow back stronger.
@ word pull up weeds | 🌿 ✋ | to take weeds out of the ground with their roots | What are you doing in the garden? | Pulling up weeds. There are so many!
@ word harvest | 🧺 🍅 | to collect ripe fruit or vegetables | When will you harvest the tomatoes? | When they turn red.
@ word repot | 🪴 🔄 | to move a plant into a new, bigger pot | My plant looks unhappy. | Maybe it needs repotting.
