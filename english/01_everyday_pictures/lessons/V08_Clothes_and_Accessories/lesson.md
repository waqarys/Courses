# 50 Clothes and Accessories with Pictures

## INTRO
Welcome to Everyday English with Pictures. What are you wearing right now? By the end of this lesson, you can answer that question in English.
You will learn fifty clothes, shoes and accessories, and some useful verbs like try on and take off. Guess each picture, then listen and repeat.

## WORDS | Tops
@ word T-shirt | 👕 | a simple top with short sleeves and no collar | What should I wear to the beach? | Just a T-shirt and shorts.
@ word shirt | 👔 | a top with a collar and buttons down the front | Is this shirt OK for the interview? | Yes, it looks smart.
@ word blouse | 👚 | a shirt for women, often light and soft | Where did you buy that blouse? | At the market. It was a bargain!
@ word sweater | 🧶 👕 | a warm top made of wool | Are you cold? | A little. I'll put on my sweater.
@ word hoodie | 🧥 | a soft, warm top with a hood | Can I borrow your hoodie? | Sure, it's on the chair.
@ word jacket | 🧥 | a short coat | Do I need a jacket tonight? | Yes, it gets cold after sunset.
@ word coat | 🧥 ❄️ | a long, warm piece of clothing you wear outside | Where's your coat? | I left it at the restaurant!
@ word blazer | 🧥 💼 | a smart jacket, often worn to the office | Do I have to wear a blazer? | Only for the meeting with clients.
@ word vest | 🎽 | a top with no sleeves | Is it hot in there? | Very. I'm only wearing a vest.
@ word raincoat | 🧥 🌧️ | a coat that keeps you dry in the rain | It's raining heavily. | Take your raincoat.

## WORDS | Bottoms and dresses
@ word jeans | 👖 | trousers made of strong blue cotton | Can I wear jeans to the office? | Only on Fridays.
@ word trousers | 👖 💼 | clothes that cover each leg separately; Americans say pants | Do these trousers fit? | They're a bit long.
@ word shorts | 🩳 | short trousers that end above the knee | Why are you wearing shorts? | It's thirty-five degrees outside!
@ word skirt | 👗 | a piece of clothing for women that hangs from the waist | Do you like this skirt? | Yes, the colour suits you.
@ word dress | 👗 | one piece of clothing for women that covers the body and the legs | What will you wear to the wedding? | A long red dress.
@ word suit | 🕴️ | a jacket and trousers or a skirt made from the same cloth | Is he wearing a suit? | Yes, he has an important meeting.
@ word tracksuit | 🏃 👕 | comfortable sports clothes: a jacket and trousers | Are you going to the gym? | Yes, that's why I'm in my tracksuit.
@ word pyjamas | 👕 🌙 | clothes you wear in bed | Why are you still in your pyjamas? | It's Sunday!
@ word sari | 🥻 | a long piece of cloth wrapped around the body, worn in South Asia | You look beautiful in that sari. | Thank you, it was my mother's.
@ word swimsuit | 🩱 | clothes you wear for swimming | Don't forget your swimsuit. | It's already in my bag.

## WORDS | Shoes and socks
@ word shoes | 👞 | things you wear on your feet, made of leather or other strong material | Nice shoes! | Thanks, they're new.
@ word sneakers | 👟 | soft sports shoes; in British English, trainers | Which shoes are best for walking? | Your sneakers, definitely.
@ word sandals | 👡 | light open shoes with straps | Can I wear sandals to the restaurant? | Yes, it's very casual.
@ word flip-flops | 🩴 | simple rubber sandals held between your toes | Where are my flip-flops? | By the pool.
@ word boots | 🥾 | strong shoes that cover your foot and part of your leg | Do I need boots for the hike? | Yes, the path is rocky.
@ word high heels | 👠 | women's shoes with a very high back part | Are you wearing high heels tonight? | No, my feet still hurt from last time.
@ word slippers | 🥿 | soft shoes you wear inside the house | My feet are cold. | Put your slippers on.
@ word socks | 🧦 | soft things you wear on your feet inside shoes | Why are your socks different colours? | I got dressed in the dark!
@ word shoelaces | 👟 🪢 | the strings that tie your shoes | Your shoelace is undone. | Oh, thanks, I'll tie it.
@ word ballet flats | 🥿 💃 | flat, simple shoes for women | Are those comfortable? | Very. Ballet flats are perfect for work.

## WORDS | Accessories
@ word cap | 🧢 | a soft hat with a hard front part | Why are you wearing a cap? | To keep the sun out of my eyes.
@ word hat | 👒 | something you wear on your head | That hat is huge! | It's great for the beach.
@ word scarf | 🧣 | a long piece of cloth you wear around your neck | Is it cold out? | Yes, take a scarf.
@ word gloves | 🧤 | things you wear on your hands to keep them warm | My hands are freezing. | You can borrow my gloves.
@ word belt | 🪢 👖 | a strap of leather or cloth worn around the waist | Your trousers are falling down! | I forgot my belt.
@ word tie | 👔 | a long, thin piece of cloth worn around the neck with a shirt | Should I wear a tie? | Not for this office.
@ word handbag | 👜 | a bag for carrying small things like keys and a wallet | Where are the keys? | In my handbag, the side pocket.
@ word backpack | 🎒 | a bag you carry on your back | Is that a new backpack? | Yes, it has a pocket for my laptop.
@ word sunglasses | 🕶️ | dark glasses that protect your eyes from the sun | It's so bright today. | Here, wear my sunglasses.
@ word watch | ⌚ | a small clock you wear on your wrist | What time is it? | Sorry, I'm not wearing a watch.

## WORDS | Jewellery and clothes verbs
@ word earrings | 💎 👂 | jewellery you wear on your ears | I love your earrings! | Thanks, my sister made them.
@ word necklace | 📿 | jewellery you wear around your neck | Is that necklace gold? | No, it's silver.
@ word ring | 💍 | jewellery you wear on your finger | Is that a wedding ring? | Yes, we got married last year.
@ word bracelet | 📿 ✋ | jewellery you wear around your wrist | Where did you get your bracelet? | It was a gift from a friend.
@ word put on | 🧥 ⬆️ | to start wearing clothes | It's cold outside. | Put on your jacket, then.
@ word take off | 👟 ⬇️ | to remove clothes or shoes | Please take off your shoes inside. | Of course, no problem.
@ word try on | 🪞 👗 | to put on clothes in a shop to see if they fit | Can I try on these jeans? | Sure, the changing room is over there.
@ word fit | 👖 ✅ | to be the right size | Do the shoes fit? | Perfectly. I'll take them.
@ word suit you | 🪞 😊 | to look good on you | Does this colour suit me? | Yes, blue really suits you.
@ word get changed | 👕 🔄 | to take off your clothes and put on different ones | Are you ready? | Give me five minutes to get changed.
