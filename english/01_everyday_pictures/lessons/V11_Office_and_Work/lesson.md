# 30 Office and Work Words with Pictures

## INTRO
Welcome to Everyday English with Pictures. Today we go to the office! You will learn thirty words for work: the people, the things on your desk, and the words you hear every day at your job.
Guess the picture, listen, repeat, and learn how to use each word in a real conversation at work.

## WORDS | People at work
@ word colleague | 🧑‍💼 🧑‍💼 | a person you work with | Who's that? | That's Ravi, my colleague from the sales team.
@ word manager | 🧑‍💼 ⭐ | the person in charge of a team | Who do I ask about a day off? | Ask your manager.
@ word employee | 🧑‍💼 | a person who is paid to work for a company | How many employees does the company have? | About two hundred.
@ word client | 🤝 🧑‍💼 | a person or company that pays for a service | Who are you meeting today? | A new client from Singapore.
@ word receptionist | 💁 🛎️ | the person at the front desk who welcomes visitors | Where do I sign in? | Ask the receptionist at the front desk.
@ word intern | 🧑‍🎓 💼 | a student or new worker getting work experience | Is he a new employee? | No, he's an intern for the summer.
@ word team | 👥 | a group of people who work together | Which team are you in? | The marketing team.
@ word boss | 🧑‍💼 👑 | the person who tells you what to do at work | Is your boss strict? | No, she's very fair.
@ word interview | om:E24A | a meeting where someone asks you questions for a job | How did the interview go? | Really well, I think.
@ word work from home | 🏠 💻 | to do your job from your house | Are you in the office tomorrow? | No, I work from home on Fridays.

## WORDS | Around the office
@ word desk | 🪑 💻 | a table where you work | Where do you sit? | At the desk by the window.
@ word laptop | 💻 | a small computer you can carry | Did you bring your laptop? | Yes, it's in my bag.
@ word printer | 🖨️ | a machine that prints documents on paper | The printer isn't working. | It's out of paper again.
@ word photocopier | 🖨️ 📄 | a machine that makes copies of documents | Where's the photocopier? | Next to the kitchen.
@ word stapler | 📎 🗂️ | a small tool that joins papers with a metal staple | Can I borrow your stapler? | Sure, here you go.
@ word sticky note | 🗒️ 🟨 | a small piece of paper with glue on the back | What's this sticky note on my screen? | A reminder: call the client at three.
@ word whiteboard | 🧑‍🏫 ⬜ | a white board you write on with special pens | Can you explain the plan? | Sure, let me draw it on the whiteboard.
@ word meeting room | 🚪 👥 | a room where people have meetings | Is the meeting room free? | No, it's booked until twelve.
@ word folder | 📁 | a cover for keeping papers together | Where's the contract? | In the blue folder on my desk.
@ word headset | 🎧 🎤 | headphones with a microphone, for calls | Can you hear me? | Yes, your new headset is great.

## WORDS | Work words
@ word deadline | ⏰ 📅 | the time or date when something must be finished | When's the deadline for the report? | Friday at five.
@ word schedule | 🗓️ | a plan of what you will do and when | What's your schedule like tomorrow? | Busy until lunch, free after that.
@ word salary | 💰 🧑‍💼 | the money you earn for your job, usually every month | Did you ask about the salary? | Yes, it's better than my current job.
@ word payslip | 🧾 💰 | a paper that shows how much you were paid | Where can I see my tax? | It's on your payslip.
@ word overtime | 🕘 🌙 | extra hours you work after your normal time | Why are you so tired? | I worked three hours of overtime.
@ word day off | 🏖️ 📅 | a day when you don't go to work | Are you working on Monday? | No, it's my day off.
@ word promotion | 📈 🎉 | a move to a higher job in the same company | Why are you celebrating? | I got a promotion!
@ word presentation | 📊 🧑‍🏫 | a talk where you show information to a group | Are you nervous about the presentation? | A little, but I've practised.
@ word report | 📄 📊 | a document that gives information about a subject | Have you finished the report? | Almost, I just need to check the numbers.
@ word resign | ✉️ 🚪 | to tell your company you are leaving your job | Why did she resign? | She got a better offer.
