# 30 Shopping and Money Words with Pictures

## INTRO
Welcome to Everyday English with Pictures. Today we go shopping! You will learn thirty words you need in a supermarket, a shop, or when you pay for something.
Guess the picture first, then listen and repeat. Each word comes with a real question and answer you can use next time you go shopping.

## WORDS | In the supermarket
@ word supermarket | 🏬 🛒 | a large shop that sells food and things for the home | Where do you buy your groceries? | At the supermarket near my office.
@ word trolley | 🛒 | a large metal basket on wheels | Do we need a trolley? | No, a basket is enough. We only need a few things.
@ word basket | 🧺 | a container with a handle, for carrying a few things | Can I have a basket, please? | They're next to the entrance.
@ word aisle | 🏪 ↔️ | the path between the shelves in a shop | Excuse me, where's the rice? | Aisle four, next to the pasta.
@ word shelf | 🗄️ 🥫 | a flat board where products are placed | Is there any more bread? | Sorry, the shelf is empty.
@ word frozen food | 🧊 🍕 | food that is kept very cold, below zero | Where's the ice cream? | In the frozen food section.
@ word fresh produce | 🥬 🍎 | fresh fruit and vegetables | Are these tomatoes local? | Yes, all our fresh produce comes from local farms.
@ word best before date | 📅 🥛 | the date until which food is at its best | Is this milk OK? | Check the best before date first.
@ word special offer | 🏷️ ⭐ | a lower price for a short time | Why did you buy three? | They were on special offer: buy two, get one free.
@ word queue | 🚶 🚶 🚶 | a line of people waiting for something | Is this the queue for the checkout? | Yes, the end is over there.

## WORDS | Paying
@ word checkout | 🏪 🧾 | the place where you pay in a supermarket | Where do I pay? | At the checkout, near the exit.
@ word cashier | 💁 🏪 | the person who takes your money in a shop | Did the cashier give you a bag? | No, I brought my own.
@ word price tag | 🏷️ | a small label that shows the price | How much is this jacket? | There's no price tag on it.
@ word cash | 💵 | money in coins and notes | Do you take cash? | Yes, cash or card is fine.
@ word coin | 🪙 | a small, round, flat piece of metal money | Do you have a coin for the trolley? | Here, take this one.
@ word card machine | 💳 📟 | the machine you use to pay by card | Can I pay by card? | Of course, just tap it on the card machine.
@ word receipt | 🧾 | the piece of paper that shows what you paid | Would you like your receipt? | Yes, please. I might return the shirt.
@ word change | 🪙 💵 | the money you get back when you pay more than the price | Here's twenty. | And here's your change, five dollars.
@ word wallet | 👛 | a small flat case for money and cards | I can't find my wallet! | Did you leave it at the checkout?
@ word shopping bag | 🛍️ | a bag for carrying the things you buy | Do you need a bag? | No thanks, I have a shopping bag.

## WORDS | Money words
@ word discount | 🏷️ ➖ | money taken off the normal price | Is there a discount for students? | Yes, ten percent with your card.
@ word sale | 🏷️ 🔥 | a time when a shop sells things at lower prices | Where did you get those shoes? | In the sale. Half price!
@ word refund | 💵 🔄 | money given back when you return something | This phone doesn't work. | You can return it for a full refund.
@ word exchange | 🔄 👕 | to give something back and get a different one | This shirt is too small. | Can I exchange it for a large?
@ word bargain | 👍 💰 | something that costs much less than usual | Only five dollars? | Yes, it's a real bargain.
@ word expensive | 💰 😮 | costing a lot of money | Why didn't you buy the watch? | It was too expensive.
@ word cheap | 💲 🙂 | not costing much money | Where do you buy fruit? | At the market. It's cheap and fresh.
@ word budget | 📒 💰 | the amount of money you plan to spend | What's your budget for a new phone? | Around three hundred dollars.
@ word save money | 🐷 🪙 | to keep money for later instead of spending it | How do you save money? | I cook at home and bring my lunch.
@ word owe | 🤝 💵 | to need to pay money back to someone | How much do I owe you for the tickets? | Twenty dollars, but no hurry.
