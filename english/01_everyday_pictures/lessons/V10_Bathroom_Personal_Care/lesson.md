# 30 Bathroom and Personal Care Verbs with Pictures

## INTRO
Welcome to Everyday English with Pictures. Today we go into the bathroom and learn thirty verbs for looking after your body: washing, brushing, shaving and more.
These are words you use every single day. Guess the picture, listen, and repeat out loud.

## WORDS | Teeth and face
@ word brush your teeth | 🪥 | to clean your teeth with a toothbrush and toothpaste | Did you brush your teeth? | Yes, for two whole minutes.
@ word floss | 🦷 🧵 | to clean between your teeth with a special thread | Do you floss every day? | I try to, at night.
@ word rinse your mouth | 💧 👄 | to wash your mouth with water and spit it out | What should I do after brushing? | Rinse your mouth with water.
@ word gargle | 💦 😮 | to move liquid around in your throat without swallowing | My throat hurts. | Gargle with warm salt water.
@ word spit | 💦 | to push liquid out of your mouth | Don't swallow the mouthwash! | I know, I spit it out.
@ word wash your face | 🧼 😊 | to clean your face with water and soap | What do you do when you get home? | I wash my face first.
@ word put on lotion | 🧴 ✋ | to rub cream onto your skin to keep it soft | My hands are so dry. | Put on some lotion.
@ word apply sunscreen | 🧴 ☀️ | to put cream on your skin to protect it from the sun | Are you ready for the beach? | Almost, I just need to apply sunscreen.
@ word shave | 🪒 | to cut hair from your face or body with a razor | Did you cut yourself? | Yes, I shaved too fast.
@ word pluck | 🤏 | to pull out small hairs, for example from your eyebrows | Why are your eyes watering? | I was plucking my eyebrows.
