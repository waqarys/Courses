# 40 Sports and Exercise Verbs with Pictures

## INTRO
Welcome to Everyday English with Pictures. Today we get moving! You will learn forty verbs for sports and exercise: what you do at the gym, in the park, in the pool and on the playing field.
Guess the picture, listen, and repeat. And if you're sitting down, why not stand up and stretch while you learn?

## WORDS | Ball games
@ word kick | ⚽ 🦶 | to hit something with your foot | Can you kick the ball back? | Sure, here it comes!
@ word throw | ⚾ 💨 | to send something through the air with your hand | How far can you throw? | Not very far, honestly.
@ word catch | 🧤 ⚾ | to take hold of something moving through the air | Catch! | Got it!
@ word hit | 🏏 | to touch something hard with a bat, racket or hand | How did he score six runs? | He hit the ball over the boundary.
@ word bounce | 🏀 | to hit the ground and come back up | Why isn't the ball bouncing? | It needs more air.
@ word pass | ⚽ ➡️ | to kick or throw the ball to someone on your team | Why didn't you pass to me? | Sorry, I didn't see you.
@ word shoot | ⚽ 🥅 | to try to score by kicking or throwing the ball at the goal | Should I shoot? | Yes, the goal is open!
@ word score | 🥅 🎉 | to win a point or a goal | Who scored the goal? | Our captain, in the last minute.
@ word serve | 🎾 | to hit the ball to start a point in tennis or volleyball | Whose turn is it to serve? | Yours. It's two all.
@ word miss | 🎯 ❌ | to fail to hit or catch something | Did you score? | No, I missed by a centimetre.

## WORDS | At the gym
@ word warm up | 🔥 🏃 | to do gentle exercise to get ready for sport | Why are you jumping up and down? | I'm warming up before the run.
@ word stretch | 🙆 | to make your muscles long to relax them | My legs hurt after running. | Did you stretch afterwards?
@ word lift weights | 🏋️ | to pick up heavy weights to make your muscles strong | What do you do at the gym? | I lift weights three times a week.
@ word do push-ups | 💪 🤸 | to push your body up from the floor with your arms | How many push-ups can you do? | About twenty.
@ word do sit-ups | 🧘 ⬆️ | to sit up from lying on your back, to make your stomach strong | Does your stomach hurt? | Yes, I did a hundred sit-ups yesterday.
@ word work out | 🏋️ 💦 | to do exercise to stay fit and healthy | You look fit! | Thanks, I work out every morning.
@ word sweat | 💦 😅 | to have water come out of your skin when you are hot | Why are you so wet? | I've been sweating on the treadmill.
@ word train | 🏃 📅 | to practise a sport regularly to get better | How often does the team train? | Four evenings a week.
@ word cool down | 🧊 🧘 | to do slow, gentle exercise after hard exercise | Should we stop now? | Let's cool down with a slow walk first.
@ word skip | 🪢 🤸 | to jump over a rope as it turns | Is skipping good exercise? | Very good. Ten minutes is hard work!

## WORDS | Moving fast
@ word jog | 🏃 | to run slowly for exercise | Do you want to go jogging tomorrow? | Sure, how about six thirty?
@ word sprint | 🏃 💨 | to run very fast for a short distance | Why are you out of breath? | I sprinted to catch the bus.
@ word cycle | 🚴 | to ride a bicycle | How do you get to work? | I cycle when it's not raining.
@ word jump | 🤾 ⬆️ | to push yourself off the ground with your legs | How high can you jump? | Not as high as you!
@ word climb | 🧗 | to go up something using your hands and feet | Have you ever climbed a mountain? | Only a small one.
@ word hike | 🥾 ⛰️ | to go on a long walk in the countryside | What did you do at the weekend? | We hiked up to the waterfall.
@ word race | 🏁 🏃 | to compete to see who is fastest | Shall we race to the tree? | OK, ready, steady, go!
@ word skate | ⛸️ | to move on ice or the ground on skates | Can you skate? | Yes, but I fall a lot!
@ word ski | ⛷️ | to move over snow on long, narrow boards | Where did you learn to ski? | In the mountains, last winter.
@ word ride | 🏇 | to sit on and control a horse, bike or motorbike | Can you ride a horse? | I tried once. It was scary.

## REVIEW | Review 3: name the pictures

## WORDS | In the water and winning
@ word swim | 🏊 | to move through water using your arms and legs | Can you swim? | Yes, I learned when I was six.
@ word dive | 🤿 🌊 | to jump into water head first | Can you dive from that high board? | No way, it's too high!
@ word float | 🛟 🌊 | to stay on top of the water without sinking | How do you float on your back? | Relax and keep your body flat.
@ word row | 🚣 | to move a boat through water with oars | Are you tired? | Yes, we rowed across the whole lake.
@ word surf | 🏄 | to ride on waves on a board | Do you surf? | I'm learning. I spend most of the time in the water!
@ word win | 🏆 | to be the best in a game or competition | Who won the match? | We did, three goals to one.
@ word lose | 😞 🥈 | to not win | Did your team win? | No, we lost in the final.
@ word draw | 🤝 ⚽ | to finish a game with the same score | What was the result? | It was a draw, two all.
@ word cheer | 📣 🙌 | to shout to support a team or player | Why is the crowd so loud? | They're cheering for the home team.
@ word referee | 🧑‍⚖️ ⚽ | to be in charge of a game and make sure players follow the rules | Who refereed the match? | A woman from the national league.
