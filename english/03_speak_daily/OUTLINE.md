# Series 3: Speak English Daily (12 conversations, season 1)

Teen and adult learners, A2-B1. NOT made for kids. Each lesson is one original, real-life conversation (14-17 lines,
two Kokoro voices with names and avatars), played in passes: LISTEN (whole dialogue), UNDERSTAND (line by line, key
phrases highlighted, a short explanation), SHADOW (listen, then repeat each line in a timed pause), ROLE-PLAY twice
(the viewer speaks one character's lines in timed pauses, then the other's), a 3-question comprehension quiz, and
8 useful phrases with listen-and-repeat. Estimated 11-15 min (C01 rendered on the box: 11.6 min). 36 Shorts
("say this in English": one phrase or dialogue line, repeat pause, meaning).
