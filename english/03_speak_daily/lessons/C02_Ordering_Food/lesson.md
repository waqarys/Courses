# Ordering Food at a Restaurant

## INTRO
Welcome to Speak English Daily. Today, we're eating out.
You'll hear Arjun ordering dinner, and Sophie, the waiter. Listen first, then understand it line by line, then repeat, and finally, play a role yourself.
Speak out loud. Even a whisper is better than silence.

## DIALOGUE | At the restaurant
@ cast A = Arjun (am_michael) | B = Sophie (bf_emma)
@ B Good evening. **Do you have a reservation?**
A reservation means you booked a table before you came.
@ A No, we don't. **Have you got a table for two?**
Have you got a table for two? The simplest way to ask for a table.
@ B Yes, of course. Follow me, please. Here's the menu.
@ A Thanks. **Could we have a few minutes?**
Could we have a few minutes? Use this when you're not ready to order yet.
@ B Sure. **Are you ready to order?**
@ A Yes. **I'd like the grilled chicken**, please.
I'd like is the polite way to order. Not, I want.
@ A **Is the curry very spicy?**
@ B It's medium. **We can make it mild** if you like.
Mild means not spicy. We can make it mild: the restaurant can change the dish for you.
@ A Then **I'll have the curry**, mild, please. **Could I get it without onions?**
I'll have is another natural way to order. Without onions is a special request.
@ B No problem. **Anything to drink?**
@ A Just a fresh lime soda, and a bottle of water, please.
@ B **Is everything okay with your meal?**
Waiters often check during the meal. A short answer is fine.
@ A It's delicious, thanks.
@ A Excuse me. **Could we have the bill, please?**
Could we have the bill? In American English, you can also say, the check.
@ B Of course. **Would you like to pay by card or cash?**
@ A **By card, please.** And thank you, everything was lovely.

Shadowing time. Listen to each line, then say it out loud. Copy the speed and the friendly tone.

## ROLEPLAY | A
Your turn. You are Arjun, the customer. When you see the microphone, order your food.

## ROLEPLAY | B
Now you are Sophie, the waiter. Say Sophie's lines.

## QUIZ | Did you understand?

@ q [🌶️] How did Arjun want his curry? | very spicy | medium | *mild
Mild, and without onions.
@ q [💳] How did he pay? | cash | *by card | he didn't pay

## PHRASES | Useful phrases for restaurants
@ phrase Have you got a table for two? | asking for a table
@ phrase I'd like the grilled chicken, please. | ordering politely
@ phrase What do you recommend? | asking the waiter for an idea
@ phrase Could I get it without onions? | a special request
@ phrase Is it vegetarian? | asking about a dish
@ phrase Could I have some more water, please? | asking for more
@ phrase Could we have the bill, please? | asking for the bill
@ phrase Can we split the bill? | each person pays for their own food
