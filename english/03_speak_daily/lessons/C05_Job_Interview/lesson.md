# Job Interview: Introduce Yourself

## INTRO
Welcome to Speak English Daily. Today, a job interview.
Neha is applying for a job as a marketing assistant. Mr Clarke is interviewing her. Listen to her answers: they're short, clear and confident.
Then you'll repeat them, and practise your own interview.

## DIALOGUE | The interview
@ cast A = Neha (af_sarah) | B = Mr Clarke (bm_daniel)
@ B Good morning, Neha. Thanks for coming in. **Tell me a little about yourself.**
Tell me about yourself. Almost every interview starts with this. Answer in three parts: now, past, and why this job.
@ A **I'm a marketing graduate**, and for the last two years **I've been working at** a small travel company.
I've been working at: something that started in the past and continues now.
@ A **I'm responsible for** our social media and our monthly newsletter.
I'm responsible for: what your job includes.
@ B **Why do you want to work for us?**
@ A **I've followed your brand for years**, and I'd like to work on bigger campaigns. I think I can learn a lot here.
Show that you know the company and that you want to grow.
@ B **What would you say is your greatest strength?**
@ A **I'm very organised.** I plan my week carefully, and I always meet deadlines.
Give a strength, then a short example that proves it.
@ B And **a weakness?**
@ A **Sometimes I find it hard to say no** to extra work. **I'm learning to** prioritise better.
A good weakness answer: a real weakness, and what you're doing about it.
@ B **Can you give me an example** of a problem you solved?
@ A Last year, our website traffic dropped. **I suggested** short videos, and **within three months**, visits went up by forty percent.
Problem, action, result. Numbers make your example stronger.
@ B Impressive. **Do you have any questions for us?**
@ A Yes. **What would a typical day look like** in this role?
Always ask a question at the end. It shows you're interested.
@ B You'd work closely with our content team. We'll **be in touch** by Friday.
Be in touch means contact you.
@ A **Thank you for your time.** I look forward to hearing from you.
I look forward to hearing from you. A polite, professional goodbye.

## LISTEN
Listen to the whole interview. Notice how calm and clear Neha sounds.

## UNDERSTAND
Now let's look at each answer and why it works.

## SHADOW
Shadowing. Repeat each line. Speak slowly and confidently: that's how good interview answers sound.

## ROLEPLAY | A
Your turn. You are Neha, the candidate. When you see the microphone, give her answer.
Later, try changing the details to your own life.

## ROLEPLAY | B
Now you are Mr Clarke, the interviewer. Ask the questions.

## QUIZ | Did you understand?
@ q [💼] How long has Neha been working at the travel company? | six months | *two years | five years
Two years.
@ q [📅] What is her greatest strength? | she's creative | *she's very organised | she speaks three languages
She's very organised, and she always meets deadlines.
@ q [📈] What happened after her idea? | sales went down | *website visits went up by forty percent | she got a new job
Visits went up by forty percent, within three months.

## PHRASES | Useful interview phrases
@ phrase I've been working as a marketing assistant for two years. | talking about your experience
@ phrase I'm responsible for our social media. | what your job includes
@ phrase My greatest strength is that I'm very organised. | talking about a strength
@ phrase Sometimes I find it hard to say no, but I'm learning to prioritise. | answering the weakness question
@ phrase For example, last year I... | starting an example
@ phrase I'd like to grow in a bigger team. | why you want the job
@ phrase What would a typical day look like in this role? | a good question for the interviewer
@ phrase I look forward to hearing from you. | a professional goodbye
