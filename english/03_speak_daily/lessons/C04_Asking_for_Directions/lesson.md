# Asking for Directions in English

## INTRO
Welcome to Speak English Daily. Today: asking for directions.
Leo is new in the city and he's looking for the train station. Grace helps him. Listen, understand, repeat, then speak in a role.
Directions are short and fast, so listen carefully.

## DIALOGUE | Where's the station?
@ cast A = Leo (am_adam) | B = Grace (af_nicole)
@ A **Excuse me, sorry to bother you.** Could you help me?
Sorry to bother you. A polite way to start talking to a stranger.
@ B Sure. What are you looking for?
@ A **How do I get to the train station?**
How do I get to? The most common way to ask for directions.
@ B Okay. **Go straight on** until you reach the traffic lights. **Then turn left.**
Go straight on means don't turn. Americans often say, go straight ahead.
@ A Straight on, then left at the lights.
Repeating the directions back is a smart way to check you understood.
@ B That's right. Then **take the second turning on the right.**
The second turning on the right: not the first street, the next one.
@ A **Sorry, could you say that again?** The second on the right?
Could you say that again? Always fine to ask. People are happy to repeat.
@ B Yes, the second on the right. The station is **at the end of that road**, **opposite the bank.**
Opposite means facing it, on the other side of the street.
@ A **Is it far from here?**
@ B Not really. **It's about a ten-minute walk.**
A ten-minute walk: it takes ten minutes on foot.
@ A **Can I get a bus there?**
@ B You can, but walking is quicker at this time of day. The traffic is terrible.
@ A Great, I'll walk. **Thanks so much for your help.**
@ B **No worries.** Good luck!
No worries is a friendly way to say, you're welcome.

## LISTEN
Listen to the whole conversation. Try to draw the route in your head.

## SHADOW
Shadowing. Listen and repeat each line. Say the directions clearly.

## ROLEPLAY | A
Your turn. You are Leo. Ask for directions when you see the microphone.

## ROLEPLAY | B
Now you are Grace. Give the directions.

## QUIZ | Did you understand?
@ q [🚦] What should Leo do at the traffic lights? | turn right | *turn left | go straight on
Turn left at the lights.
@ q [🏦] Where is the station? | next to the bank | *opposite the bank | behind the bank
Opposite the bank, at the end of the road.
@ q [🚶] How long is the walk? | five minutes | *ten minutes | thirty minutes
About a ten-minute walk.

## PHRASES | Useful phrases for directions
@ phrase Excuse me, how do I get to the station? | asking for directions
@ phrase Is there a pharmacy near here? | asking about a place nearby
@ phrase Go straight on, then turn left. | giving simple directions
@ phrase Take the second turning on the right. | which street to take
@ phrase Sorry, could you say that again? | asking someone to repeat
@ phrase Is it far from here? | asking about distance
@ phrase It's about a ten-minute walk. | saying how far it is
@ phrase Could you show me on the map? | asking for help with a map
