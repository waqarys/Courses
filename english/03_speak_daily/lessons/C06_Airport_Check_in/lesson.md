# At the Airport Check-in

## INTRO
Welcome to Speak English Daily. Today, we're at the airport.
Ravi is flying to Toronto, and Emma works at the check-in desk. Listen, understand, repeat, then speak in a role.
Airport English is fast, so these phrases will really help.

## DIALOGUE | At the check-in desk
@ cast A = Ravi (am_michael) | B = Emma (bf_isabella)
@ B Good morning. **Where are you flying to today?**
@ A To Toronto. **Here's my passport** and my booking reference.
A booking reference is the code you get when you book a ticket.
@ B Thank you. **Are you checking in any bags?**
To check in a bag means to give it to the airline. It goes under the plane.
@ A Yes, **just this suitcase.** And this backpack is **my hand luggage.**
Hand luggage is the small bag you take on the plane with you.
@ B Could you put the suitcase on the scale, please? Oh, **it's two kilos over the limit.**
Over the limit: too heavy for the rules.
@ A Oh no. **Can I move some things** into my backpack?
@ B Sure, go ahead.
@ A Okay. **Is that better?**
@ B Perfect, it's fine now. **Did you pack this bag yourself?**
Airlines ask this for safety. The answer should be yes.
@ A Yes, I did.
@ B **Would you like a window or an aisle seat?**
A window seat is next to the window. An aisle seat is next to the passage, so it's easy to get up.
@ A **An aisle seat, please.** It's a long flight.
@ B Here's your **boarding pass.** **Boarding starts at ten fifteen**, from **gate twenty-two.**
The gate is the door where you get on the plane. Boarding is getting on.
@ A **Is the flight on time?**
@ B At the moment, **it's delayed by about thirty minutes.** Please check the screens.
Delayed means late.
@ A Okay, thanks for your help.
@ B **Have a pleasant flight.**

## QUIZ | Did you understand?
@ q [🧳] What was the problem with the suitcase? | it was broken | *it was two kilos too heavy | it was the wrong colour
It was two kilos over the limit.
@ q [💺] Which seat did Ravi choose? | a window seat | *an aisle seat | a middle seat
An aisle seat, because it's a long flight.
@ q [🕙] How late is the flight? | it's on time | ten minutes | *about thirty minutes
About thirty minutes.

## PHRASES | Useful airport phrases
@ phrase I'd like to check in for my flight to Toronto. | starting at the desk
@ phrase I'm checking in one bag. | saying how many bags
@ phrase Could I have an aisle seat, please? | choosing your seat
@ phrase What's the baggage allowance? | asking how much luggage you can take
@ phrase Which gate do I go to? | asking about the gate
@ phrase Is the flight delayed? | asking about the time
@ phrase Where's the security check? | finding security
@ phrase My bag didn't arrive. | reporting lost luggage
