# At the Doctor's

## INTRO
Welcome to Speak English Daily. Today, we're at the doctor's.
You'll hear a short conversation between Maya and Doctor Rao. First, just listen. Then we'll go through it line by line. Then you'll repeat every line, and at the end, you'll take a role and speak yourself.
Get ready to speak out loud. It's the fastest way to learn.

## DIALOGUE | At the clinic
@ cast A = Maya (af_heart) | B = Dr Rao (bm_george)
@ B Good morning. Please, have a seat. **What seems to be the problem?**
What seems to be the problem? This is how a doctor politely asks, what's wrong?
@ A Good morning, doctor. **I've had a sore throat** and a headache for three days.
I've had a sore throat. Use I've had for a problem that started in the past and is still there.
@ B I see. **Do you have a temperature?**
Do you have a temperature? This means, do you have a fever?
@ A Yes, a little. It was thirty-eight degrees last night.
@ B Any cough or runny nose?
@ A **A dry cough**, mostly at night. It keeps me awake.
A dry cough has no mucus. It keeps me awake means I can't sleep because of it.
@ B Let me have a look. Open your mouth and say, ah.
@ B Your throat is quite red. It looks like **a throat infection**.
@ A **Is it serious?**
Is it serious? A short, natural question when you're worried.
@ B No, don't worry. I'll give you some tablets. **Take one twice a day, after meals.**
Twice a day means two times every day. After meals means after you eat.
@ A **Should I avoid anything?**
Should I avoid anything? A good way to ask what not to eat, drink or do.
@ B Avoid cold drinks, and **get plenty of rest**. Drink lots of warm water.
@ A How long will it take to get better?
@ B About five days. **If you don't feel better by then, come back and see me.**
If you don't feel better by then, come back. The doctor tells you when to return.
@ A Thank you, doctor. **I really appreciate it.**
I really appreciate it. A warm, polite way to say thank you.

## LISTEN
Listen to the whole conversation. Don't read too hard. Just try to follow the story.

## UNDERSTAND
Now let's go through it line by line. Look at the highlighted phrases.

## SHADOW
Now, shadowing. Listen to each line, then say it out loud, at the same speed. Copy the music of the sentence, not just the words.

## ROLEPLAY | A
Your turn. You are Maya, the patient. When you see the microphone, say Maya's line out loud.

## ROLEPLAY | B
Now switch. You are Doctor Rao. Say the doctor's lines.

## QUIZ | Did you understand?
@ q [🤒] How long has Maya had a sore throat? | one day | *three days | a week
Three days. She said, I've had a sore throat and a headache for three days.
@ q [💊] How often should she take the tablets? | once a day | *twice a day | three times a day
Twice a day, after meals.
@ q [🥤] What should Maya avoid? | warm water | *cold drinks | rest
Cold drinks. And she should get plenty of rest.

## PHRASES | Useful phrases at the doctor's
@ phrase I've had a sore throat for three days. | describing a symptom and how long
@ phrase I've got a terrible headache. | another way to describe a symptom
@ phrase It started on Monday. | saying when the problem started
@ phrase I feel dizzy when I stand up. | describing when it happens
@ phrase Is it serious? | asking if you should worry
@ phrase How often should I take this? | asking about medicine
@ phrase Are there any side effects? | asking what problems the medicine can cause
@ phrase Do I need to come back? | asking about a follow-up visit
