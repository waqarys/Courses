# At the Pharmacy

## INTRO
Welcome to Speak English Daily. Today, we're at the pharmacy. In British English, people also say, the chemist's.
Omar has itchy eyes and he keeps sneezing. Ms Lee, the pharmacist, helps him choose something. Listen, understand, repeat, then speak in a role.

## DIALOGUE | At the counter
@ cast A = Omar (am_adam) | B = Ms Lee (bf_emma)
@ B Hello. **What can I get for you?**
@ A Hi. **Can you recommend something for hay fever?**
Hay fever is an allergy to pollen from grass and flowers. Can you recommend something for: very useful at any pharmacy.
@ B Of course. **What are your symptoms?**
Symptoms are the signs of an illness, like a cough or a fever.
@ A **My eyes are itchy**, and **I keep sneezing.** It's worse in the mornings.
I keep sneezing: it happens again and again.
@ B **How long have you had it?**
@ A **Since last week.** It started when the weather got warmer.
@ B **Are you taking any other medicine?**
@ A No. But **I'm allergic to aspirin.**
Always tell the pharmacist about allergies. I'm allergic to: my body reacts badly to it.
@ B Thanks for telling me. These tablets are fine for you. **You don't need a prescription.**
A prescription is a note from a doctor. Without one, you can buy medicine over the counter.
@ A **How often should I take them?**
@ B **One tablet a day**, ideally in the evening.
@ A **Do they have any side effects?**
Side effects are other things a medicine can do, besides helping you.
@ B **They might make you a little drowsy.** So **don't drive** after taking one.
Drowsy means sleepy.
@ A Okay. **And these eye drops**, how do I use them?
@ B **Two drops in each eye**, up to three times a day.
@ B **If it doesn't get better in a week, see a doctor.**
@ A Great, thank you. **How much is that altogether?**
Altogether means the total for everything.
@ B That's twelve dollars, please.

@ q [🤧] What is Omar's problem? | a sore throat | *hay fever | a headache
Hay fever: itchy eyes and sneezing.
@ q [⚠️] What is Omar allergic to? | eye drops | *aspirin | pollen tablets
Aspirin. He told the pharmacist before she chose the tablets.
@ q [😴] What side effect can the tablets have? | a headache | *they can make him drowsy | itchy eyes
They might make him drowsy, so he shouldn't drive.

## PHRASES | Useful pharmacy phrases
@ phrase Can you recommend something for hay fever? | asking for medicine
@ phrase I've got a bad cough. | describing a symptom
@ phrase I'm allergic to aspirin. | telling them about an allergy
@ phrase Do I need a prescription for this? | asking if a doctor's note is needed
@ phrase How often should I take them? | asking about the dose
@ phrase Do they have any side effects? | asking about side effects
@ phrase Can I take this with food? | asking how to take it
@ phrase How much is that altogether? | asking for the total
