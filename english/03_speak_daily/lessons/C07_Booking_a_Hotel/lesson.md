# Booking a Hotel Room

## INTRO
Welcome to Speak English Daily. Today, we're booking a hotel room by phone.
Daniel is calling the Bayview Hotel, and Aisha is at the reception desk. Phone calls can be hard, because you can't see the other person. So listen carefully.

## DIALOGUE | On the phone
@ cast A = Daniel (am_adam) | B = Aisha (af_heart)
@ B Good afternoon, Bayview Hotel. **Aisha speaking. How can I help you?**
On the phone, we say, this is, or, speaking. Not, I am Aisha.
@ A Hi. **I'd like to book a room**, please.
@ B Of course. **For which dates?**
@ A **From the twelfth to the fifteenth of March.** So, three nights.
@ B **Is that a single or a double room?**
A single room is for one person. A double room has a big bed for two.
@ A A double, please. **How much is it per night?**
Per night means for each night.
@ A **Is breakfast included?**
Included means it's part of the price. You don't pay extra.
@ B It's ninety-five dollars a night, **with breakfast included.**
@ A That sounds good. **What time is check-in?**
@ B Check-in is from two p.m., and **check-out is by eleven.**
Check-in is when you arrive and get your key. Check-out is when you leave.
@ A **Is it possible to check in early?** My train arrives at ten.
@ B **We can't promise**, but you're welcome to **leave your bags with us.**
We can't promise: maybe yes, maybe no.
@ B **Could I have your name, please?**
@ A **It's Daniel Brooks. B, R, double O, K, S.**
Spelling your name is normal on the phone. Double O means two O's.
@ B Thank you, Mr Brooks. **Your booking is confirmed.** We'll send the details by email.
@ A Great. **Thanks for your help.** Bye.

## LISTEN
Listen to the whole phone call.

## UNDERSTAND

## SHADOW
Shadowing. Listen and repeat each line.

## ROLEPLAY | A
Your turn. You are Daniel. Book the room.

## ROLEPLAY | B
Now you are Aisha, at reception. Answer the phone.

## QUIZ | Did you understand?
@ q [🌙] How many nights is Daniel staying? | two | *three | five
Three nights, from the twelfth to the fifteenth of March.
@ q [🥐] Is breakfast included? | *yes | no | only on Sunday
Yes. It's ninety-five dollars a night, with breakfast.
@ q [🕑] When is check-in? | from ten a.m. | from eleven a.m. | *from two p.m.
From two p.m. And check-out is by eleven.

## PHRASES | Useful hotel phrases
@ phrase I'd like to book a double room for three nights. | booking a room
@ phrase How much is it per night? | asking the price
@ phrase Is breakfast included? | asking what's in the price
@ phrase What time is check-out? | asking about times
@ phrase Is it possible to check in early? | arriving early
@ phrase I have a reservation under the name Brooks. | arriving at the hotel
@ phrase The air conditioning isn't working. | reporting a problem in your room
@ phrase Could I have a late check-out? | asking to leave later
