# Calling Customer Service

## INTRO
Welcome to Speak English Daily. Today, a phone call to customer service.
Sara ordered a lamp online, and it arrived broken. Mike works in customer service. Notice how Sara stays polite but clear. That's the secret to getting help.

## DIALOGUE | The phone call
@ cast A = Sara (af_nicole) | B = Mike (am_michael)
@ B Thank you for calling Homeline. **This is Mike. How can I help you today?**
@ A Hi, Mike. **I'm calling about an order** I received yesterday.
I'm calling about: the best way to say why you're calling.
@ B Sure. **Could I have your order number, please?**
@ A **Yes, it's four, seven, two, nine, one.**
Say numbers one by one, clearly.
@ B Thank you. I can see a table lamp. **What seems to be the problem?**
@ A **I'm afraid it arrived damaged.** The glass is cracked.
I'm afraid is a polite way to give bad news. Damaged means broken.
@ B **I'm really sorry to hear that.** **Could you send us a photo?**
@ A **I've already emailed one** this morning.
@ B Ah yes, I can see it. **Would you prefer a refund or a replacement?**
A refund is your money back. A replacement is a new lamp.
@ A **I'd prefer a replacement, please.** I really like the lamp.
@ B No problem. **Shall we send a courier** to collect the broken one?
A courier is a person who delivers and collects parcels.
@ A Yes, please. **When will the new one arrive?**
@ A And **could you give me a reference number?**
A reference number helps if you need to call again.
@ B Of course. It's R, M, eight, three, five. **It should arrive within three working days.**
Working days are Monday to Friday.
@ A Perfect. **Thanks for sorting this out so quickly.**
Sorting this out means solving the problem.
@ B **My pleasure.** Is there anything else I can help you with?
@ A No, that's everything. Bye!

@ q [💡] What was wrong with the lamp? | it was the wrong colour | *the glass was cracked | it never arrived
The glass was cracked. It arrived damaged.
@ q [📦] What did Sara choose? | a refund | *a replacement | a discount
A replacement. She really likes the lamp.
@ q [📅] When will the new lamp arrive? | tomorrow | *within three working days | next month
Within three working days.

## PHRASES | Useful customer service phrases
@ phrase I'm calling about an order I received yesterday. | why you're calling
@ phrase I'm afraid it arrived damaged. | explaining a problem politely
@ phrase My order number is four seven two nine one. | giving your order number
@ phrase I'd like a refund, please. | asking for your money back
@ phrase Could you send a replacement? | asking for a new one
@ phrase Could you give me a reference number? | for the next call
@ phrase I'd like to speak to a manager, please. | when you need more help
@ phrase Thanks for sorting this out. | thanking someone for solving it
