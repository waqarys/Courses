# At the Supermarket Checkout

## INTRO
Welcome to Speak English Daily. Today, we're at the supermarket.
Priya is doing her weekly shopping, and Tom is the cashier. Listen, understand, repeat, and then speak in a role.
These are phrases you can use this week.

## DIALOGUE | At the checkout
@ cast A = Priya (af_bella) | B = Tom (bm_lewis)
@ A Excuse me, **where can I find the rice?**
Where can I find? The most useful shopping question.
@ B It's **in aisle five**, next to the pasta.
An aisle is a long passage between shelves in a shop.
@ A Thanks. And **is this the queue for the checkout?**
A queue is a line of people waiting. Americans say, a line.
@ B Yes, I can take you here. Hi there. **Did you find everything you needed?**
@ A Yes, thanks.
@ B **Do you need a bag?**
@ A **No, thanks. I've brought my own.**
I've brought my own. My own means it belongs to me.
@ B Great. **Do you have a loyalty card?**
A loyalty card gives you points or discounts each time you shop.
@ A Yes, here it is. And **I have a coupon** for the coffee.
@ B Lovely. That's ten percent off. **Your total is forty-two dollars fifty.**
@ A Sorry, **I think the apples are on offer.** It said buy one, get one free.
On offer means at a special, lower price.
@ B Let me check. Oh, you're right. **I'm sorry about that.** That's thirty-nine dollars now.
@ A No problem. **Can I pay by card?**
@ B Of course. **Just tap your card here.**
To tap means to touch the card on the machine.
@ A Done. **Could I have the receipt, please?**
A receipt is the paper that shows what you bought and paid.
@ B Here you go. Have a nice day!
@ A You too. Bye!

## LISTEN
Listen to the whole conversation.

## UNDERSTAND
Now line by line. Notice how polite and short the sentences are.

## SHADOW
Shadowing. Listen, then repeat each line out loud.

## ROLEPLAY | A
Your turn. You are Priya, the customer. Speak when you see the microphone.

## ROLEPLAY | B
Now you are Tom, the cashier.

## QUIZ | Did you understand?
@ q [🍚] Where is the rice? | *aisle five | aisle nine | next to the coffee
In aisle five, next to the pasta.
@ q [🛍️] Did Priya need a bag? | yes, two bags | *no, she brought her own | the shop had no bags
No. She said, I've brought my own.
@ q [🍎] What was wrong with the price? | the coffee coupon didn't work | *the apples were on offer | she was charged twice
The apples were on offer: buy one, get one free.

## PHRASES | Useful shopping phrases
@ phrase Where can I find the rice? | asking where something is
@ phrase Do you sell phone chargers? | asking if the shop has something
@ phrase No, thanks. I've brought my own bag. | answering, do you need a bag?
@ phrase Is this on offer? | asking about a special price
@ phrase Can I pay by card? | asking how to pay
@ phrase I think there's a mistake on the bill. | politely saying the price is wrong
@ phrase Could I have the receipt, please? | asking for the receipt
@ phrase Can I return this if it doesn't fit? | asking about returns
