# Meeting a New Neighbour

## INTRO
Welcome to Speak English Daily. Today: small talk.
Ben has just moved into the flat next door. Anna, his neighbour, says hello. Small talk is easy once you know a few patterns: ask, answer, and ask back.
Let's listen.

## DIALOGUE | Next door
@ cast A = Anna (af_bella) | B = Ben (bm_george)
@ A Hi! **You must be the new neighbour.** I'm Anna, from number twelve.
You must be: I'm almost sure you are. A friendly way to start.
@ B Hi, Anna. **Nice to meet you.** I'm Ben. **I moved in on Saturday.**
Moved in means started living in a new home.
@ A **How are you settling in?**
Settling in means getting comfortable in a new place.
@ B **Not bad, thanks.** **There are still boxes everywhere**, but it's starting to feel like home.
@ A **Where did you move from?**
@ B From Manchester. **I got a new job** at the hospital here. I'm a nurse.
@ A Oh, **that must be really busy.**
That must be: a friendly guess about someone's life. It shows you're listening.
@ B **It can be**, but I love it. **What about you?** What do you do?
What about you? The easiest way to ask back and keep the conversation going.
@ A **I work from home.** I'm a graphic designer.
@ B **That sounds interesting.** Is there a good supermarket around here?
@ A Yes, **there's one just round the corner**, and **the market on Sundays is great.**
Just round the corner means very close.
@ A **If you need anything**, just knock on my door.
@ B **That's really kind of you.** Thanks.
@ A **Anyway, I'll let you get back to your unpacking.**
Anyway, I'll let you get back to: a polite way to end a conversation.
@ B **It was lovely to meet you**, Anna.
@ A You too, Ben. **See you around!**
See you around: a relaxed goodbye to someone you'll see again.

Shadowing. Listen and repeat. Smile while you say it: it really changes your voice!

## ROLEPLAY | B

@ q [🧑‍⚕️] What is Ben's job? | a graphic designer | *a nurse | a teacher

## PHRASES | Useful small talk phrases
@ phrase Hi, I'm Anna. I live at number twelve. | introducing yourself
@ phrase How are you settling in? | asking a new person how it's going
@ phrase What about you? | asking back
@ phrase That sounds interesting. | showing interest
@ phrase How long have you lived here? | a good question for neighbours
@ phrase If you need anything, just let me know. | offering help
@ phrase It was lovely to meet you. | a warm goodbye
@ phrase Anyway, I'll let you get on. | ending a chat politely
