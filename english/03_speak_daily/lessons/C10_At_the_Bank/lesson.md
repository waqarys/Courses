# At the Bank

## INTRO
Welcome to Speak English Daily. Today, we're at the bank.
Kenji has just moved to a new city for work, and he needs a bank account. Ms Patel helps him. Banks use a lot of special words, so let's learn them together.

## DIALOGUE | Opening an account
@ cast A = Kenji (am_michael) | B = Ms Patel (af_sarah)
@ B Good morning. **How can I help you today?**
@ A Hi. **I'd like to open a bank account**, please.
@ B Certainly. **Is it a current account or a savings account?**
A current account is for everyday money. A savings account is for money you keep.
@ A **A current account.** I've just started a new job, so **my salary will be paid into it.**
Paid into means the money goes into the account.
@ B Great. **Could I see some ID**, please? A passport is fine.
ID means a document that shows who you are.
@ A Here's my passport.
@ B Thank you. **I'll also need proof of address.**
@ A **What counts as proof of address?**
What counts as: what is accepted as.
@ B **A recent utility bill** or your rental agreement.
A utility bill is a bill for electricity, water or gas.
@ A **I've got my rental agreement** on my phone. Is that okay?
@ B Yes, you can email it to me.
@ A Great. **Is there a monthly fee?**
@ B **There's no fee** if you pay in at least five hundred dollars a month.
Pay in means put money into the account.
@ A Perfect. **When will I get my debit card?**
A debit card takes money straight from your account.
@ B **It'll arrive by post** within a week. **The PIN comes separately.**
Your PIN is the secret number for your card. It's sent separately for safety.
@ A **Can I set up online banking** today as well?
@ B Yes. I'll help you **download the app** before you go.
@ A Brilliant. **Thanks for your help.**
