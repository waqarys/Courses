# Making Plans with a Friend

## INTRO
Welcome to Speak English Daily. This is the last conversation of the season: making plans with a friend.
Zoe calls her friend Sam to plan the weekend. You'll hear how to invite, suggest, say no politely, and agree on a time.

## DIALOGUE | Plans for Saturday
@ cast A = Zoe (af_nicole) | B = Sam (am_adam)
@ A Hey Sam! **Are you free on Saturday?**
Are you free? The easiest way to start an invitation.
@ B Hi Zoe! **I think so.** Why, what's up?
What's up? Here it means, what's happening, why do you ask?
@ A **How about going to the new food market** by the river?
How about plus an i-n-g verb: a friendly suggestion.
@ B **That sounds great!** What time?
@ A **Shall we meet at eleven?**
Shall we? Another way to suggest something.
@ B **I'm afraid I can't do the morning.** I've got football practice.
I'm afraid I can't. A polite way to say no.
@ B **Could we make it a bit later?** Like one o'clock?
Make it later means change the time to later.
@ A **One works for me.** **Why don't we** have lunch there, then?
One works for me: one o'clock is fine. Why don't we: another suggestion.
@ B Perfect. **Should I ask Leila too?**
@ A Yes, **the more the merrier!**
The more the merrier: more people make it more fun.
@ B **Where shall we meet?**
@ A **Let's meet at the bus stop** outside the library.
Let's: a suggestion that includes you and me.
@ B Great. If I'm late, **I'll text you.**
@ A **Sounds like a plan.** See you on Saturday!
Sounds like a plan: we agree, it's decided.
@ B **See you then!**

## QUIZ | Did you understand?
@ q [⚽] Why can't Sam meet in the morning? | he's working | *he has football practice | he's sleeping
He's got football practice.
@ q [🕐] What time will they meet? | eleven | *one o'clock | three o'clock
One o'clock. They'll have lunch at the market.
@ q [🚏] Where will they meet? | at the food market | *at the bus stop outside the library | at Sam's house
At the bus stop outside the library.
