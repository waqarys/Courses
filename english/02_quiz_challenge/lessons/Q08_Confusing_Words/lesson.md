# Confusing Words Quiz

## INTRO
Welcome to the English Quiz Challenge. Today: confusing words. These words sound the same, or almost the same, but they have different spellings and meanings. Even native speakers mix them up!
Twenty questions, five seconds each. Keep your score!

## QUIZ | Questions 1 to 10
@ q ___ going to the cinema tonight. | Their | There | *They're
They're means they are. They are going to the cinema.
@ q Is that ___ car, or is it a rental? | *their | there | they're
Their shows that something belongs to them.
@ q Put the box over ___, next to the door. | their | *there | they're
There is a place.
@ q ___ late again! | Your | *You're | Yore
You're means you are. You are late again.
@ q Is this ___ phone? | *your | you're | you
Your shows that something belongs to you.
@ q ___ raining. Take an umbrella. | Its | *It's | Its'
It's means it is. It is raining.
@ q The dog wagged ___ tail. | *its | it's | it is
Its, with no apostrophe, shows that something belongs to it.
@ q We're going ___ the beach. | *to | too | two
To shows direction.
@ q This coffee is ___ hot to drink. | to | *too | two
Too means more than you want.
@ q I have ___ brothers. | to | too | *two
Two, the number two.

## QUIZ | Questions 11 to 20
@ q ___ house is the one with the red door? | Who's | *Whose | Whos
Whose asks who something belongs to. Who's means who is.
@ q I don't know ___ to go left or right. | weather | *whether | wether
Whether means if. Weather is sun and rain.
@ q Be careful not to ___ your wallet. | loose | *lose | lost
Lose, with one O, means not be able to find something. Loose, with two O's, means not tight.
@ q This shirt is too ___. I need a smaller size. | *loose | lose | lost
Loose: not tight.
@ q My brother is taller ___ me. | then | *than | that
Than is for comparing: taller than me.
@ q First we eat, ___ we watch a film. | than | *then | them
Then means after that.
@ q The weather can really ___ your mood. | *affect | effect | infect
Affect is usually a verb: the rain affects my mood.
@ q The medicine had a good ___. | affect | *effect | efect
Effect is usually a noun: a good effect.
@ q I'll have a coffee, and my friend will have tea as ___. | good | *well | better
As well means too, and it goes at the end of the sentence: my friend will have tea as well.
@ q Can I ___ your pen for a minute? | lend | *borrow | rent
Borrow means take something for a short time. Lend means give it for a short time.
