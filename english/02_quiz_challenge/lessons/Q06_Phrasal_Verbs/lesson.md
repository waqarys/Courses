# Phrasal Verbs Quiz

## INTRO
Welcome to the English Quiz Challenge. Today: phrasal verbs. A phrasal verb is a verb plus a small word, like up, off or out, and together they make a new meaning. Give up means stop trying.
Native speakers use them all the time. Twenty questions, five seconds each. Pick the phrasal verb that fits. Keep your score!

## QUIZ | Questions 1 to 10
@ q Can you ___ my plants while I'm on holiday? | look for | *look after | look up
Look after means take care of.
@ q Don't ___! You're almost at the finish line. | give in | *give up | give away
Give up means stop trying.
@ q We've ___ milk. Can you buy some? | run into | *run out of | run over
Run out of means have no more of something.
@ q Please ___ your shoes before you come in. | put on | *take off | take up
Take off means remove clothes or shoes.
@ q I don't know this word. I'll ___ in the dictionary. | *look it up | look it after | look it on
Look up means search for information.
@ q I ___ an old friend at the station today. | ran out | *ran into | ran over
Run into means meet someone by chance.
@ q The music is too loud. Can you ___? | turn it up | *turn it down | turn it over
Turn down means make the volume lower. Turn up means make it louder.
@ q What time do you usually ___ in the morning? | *get up | get on | get off
Get up means get out of bed.
@ q The meeting is ___ until next week. | put on | *put off | put up
Put off means move to a later time.
@ q I'm trying to ___ smoking. | *give up | give back | give out
Give up can also mean stop a habit.

## QUIZ | Questions 11 to 20
@ q Can you ___ the form, please? Name, address and phone number. | *fill in | fill up | fill over
Fill in means write information in the spaces on a form.
@ q Hurry up, ___ the bus! It's leaving. | *get on | get in | get up
Get on a bus, train or plane. But get in a car.
@ q Our plane ___ at six in the morning. | took up | *took off | took over
Take off, for a plane, means leave the ground.
@ q I ___ my grandmother. We have the same eyes. | take off | *take after | take on
Take after means look or behave like an older family member.
@ q Let's ___ for dinner tonight. I don't want to cook. | go off | *go out | go on
Go out means leave home to do something fun.
@ q My car ___ on the motorway. | broke up | *broke down | broke out
Break down means stop working, for a machine.
@ q Can you ___ me ___ at the airport? My flight lands at ten. | *pick, up | pick, out | pick, on
Pick up means collect someone.
@ q I can't ___ with this noise any more! | put off | *put up | put out
Put up with means accept something annoying.
@ q We need to ___ a solution before Friday. | *come up with | come down with | come along with
Come up with means think of an idea.
@ q She ___ the job offer because the salary was too low. | turned up | *turned down | turned over
Turn down an offer means refuse it.
