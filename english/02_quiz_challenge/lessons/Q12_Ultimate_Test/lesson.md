# Ultimate English Test

## INTRO
Welcome to the season finale of the English Quiz Challenge! Thirty mixed questions: vocabulary, grammar, spelling, prepositions, phrasal verbs and idioms. The first ten are easy, the next ten are medium, the last ten are hard.
Five seconds each. Pause if you need to. Get a pen, keep your score, and at the end we'll tell you your level. Good luck!

## QUIZ | Level 1: easy
@ q What is the opposite of **heavy**? | big | *light | thin
Light. A heavy bag, a light bag.
@ q She ___ to work every day. | go | *goes | going
Goes. He, she and it take S in the present simple.
@ q [🍌] What is this? | *banana | mango | lemon
A banana.
@ q I was born ___ March. | on | at | *in
In March. Months take in.
@ q Which spelling is correct? | frend | *friend | freind
Friend: F-R-I-E-N-D. There's an "end" in friend.
@ q They ___ football yesterday. | play | *played | playing
Played. Yesterday tells us it's the past.
@ q I'd like ___ apple, please. | a | *an | the
An apple.
@ q Which word means the same as **begin**? | end | *start | stop
Start.
@ q How ___ is this shirt? | many | *much | more
How much is it? We use much for money and things we can't count.
@ q [🕖] What time is it? It's ___. | *seven o'clock | seven hours | seven times
Seven o'clock.

## SCORE | Level 1 score check
That was level one. Nine or ten? Great start. Now it gets harder. Subscribe if you want a new quiz every week.

## QUIZ | Level 2: medium
@ q I have lived here ___ 2019. | for | *since | from
Since 2019. Since with a point in time, for with a period: for five years.
@ q She's good ___ maths. | in | on | *at
Good at.
@ q If it rains tomorrow, we ___ at home. | stayed | *will stay | would stay
Will stay. If plus present, then will: the first conditional.
@ q Which word is a **synonym** of **angry**? | *mad | sad | glad
Mad. In everyday American English, mad often means angry.
@ q We ran ___ sugar, so I couldn't bake. | out | *out of | off
Ran out of.
@ q This is the ___ film I've ever seen. | baddest | *worst | most bad
The worst. Bad, worse, worst.
@ q I'm looking forward ___ you. | to see | *to seeing | for seeing
To seeing. Look forward to plus verb I-N-G.
@ q Which sentence is correct? | She don't like coffee. | *She doesn't like coffee. | She not like coffee.
She doesn't like coffee.
@ q There aren't ___ eggs left. | some | much | *any
Any. In negatives and questions, we usually use any.
@ q Could you ___ me your pen? | borrow | *lend | rent
Lend. You lend to someone; you borrow from someone.

## SCORE | Level 2 score check
That was level two. If you have sixteen or more so far, you're doing really well. Now, the hard part.

## QUIZ | Level 3: hard
@ q By the time we arrived, the film ___. | started | *had started | has started
Had started: the past perfect, for the earlier of two past actions.
@ q I wish I ___ more time. | have | *had | will have
Had. I wish plus past simple, for a present situation we want to change.
@ q Which spelling is correct? | *accommodation | accomodation | acommodation
Accommodation: double C, double M.
@ q It's no use ___ about it now. | to worry | *worrying | worry
Worrying. It's no use plus verb I-N-G.
@ q **Once in a blue moon** means... | every month | *very rarely | at night
Very rarely.
@ q She suggested ___ a taxi. | to take | *taking | take
Taking. Suggest plus verb I-N-G.
@ q Neither my brother nor my sister ___ coffee. | drink | *drinks | drinking
Drinks. With neither, nor, the verb agrees with the nearer subject: my sister drinks.
@ q He's used to ___ early. | wake up | *waking up | woke up
Waking up. Be used to plus verb I-N-G means it is normal for him.
@ q **Hit the sack** means... | *go to bed | hit someone | go shopping
Go to bed.
@ q The report must ___ by Friday. | finish | *be finished | finishing
Be finished. A passive, because the report doesn't finish itself.
