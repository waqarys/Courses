# Irregular Verbs Quiz: Past Tense

## INTRO
Welcome to the English Quiz Challenge. Today: the past tense of irregular verbs. Regular verbs just add E-D: walk, walked. But irregular verbs change in their own way: go becomes went.
Twenty questions, five seconds each. Choose the correct past tense. Keep your score!

## QUIZ | Questions 1 to 10
@ q Yesterday I ___ to the market. (go) | goed | *went | gone
Went. Yesterday I went to the market. Gone is the past participle: I have gone.
@ q She ___ a new phone last week. (buy) | buyed | *bought | brought
Bought. Careful: bought comes from buy, but brought comes from bring.
@ q We ___ pizza for dinner. (eat) | eated | eaten | *ate
Ate. We ate pizza.
@ q I ___ you were at work. (think) | thinked | *thought | taught
Thought. I thought you were at work.
@ q He ___ me a funny story. (tell) | telled | *told | tolded
Told. He told me a story.
@ q They ___ the bus at seven. (take) | *took | taked | taken
Took. They took the bus.
@ q I ___ my keys at home! (leave) | leaved | *left | lefted
Left. I left my keys at home.
@ q She ___ her umbrella on the train. (forget) | forgetted | *forgot | forgotten
Forgot. She forgot her umbrella.
@ q We ___ a great film on Saturday. (see) | seed | *saw | seen
Saw. We saw a film. Seen needs have: we have seen it.
@ q He ___ the window by accident. (break) | breaked | *broke | broken
Broke. He broke the window.

## QUIZ | Questions 11 to 20
@ q I ___ eight hours last night. (sleep) | sleeped | *slept | sleept
Slept. I slept eight hours.
@ q She ___ the race! (win) | winned | *won | wan
Won. She won the race. It sounds like the number one.
@ q I ___ a strange noise outside. (hear) | heared | *heard | hord
Heard. I heard a noise.
@ q My mother ___ me to cook. (teach) | teached | *taught | thought
Taught. My mother taught me. Teach, taught, taught.
@ q He ___ his wallet in the taxi. (lose) | losed | *lost | loosed
Lost. He lost his wallet.
@ q We ___ in a small hotel. (stay) | *stayed | stood | stay
Stayed. Stay is a regular verb! Not every verb is irregular.
@ q They ___ a lot of money on the trip. (spend) | spended | *spent | spand
Spent. They spent a lot of money.
@ q She ___ me a birthday card. (send) | sended | *sent | sand
Sent. She sent me a card.
@ q I ___ the last train home. (catch) | catched | *caught | cought
Caught. I caught the last train.
@ q He ___ his brother to the party. (bring) | bringed | bought | *brought
Brought. He brought his brother. Bring, brought, brought.
