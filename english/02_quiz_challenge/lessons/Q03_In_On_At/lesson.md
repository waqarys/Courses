# In, On or At? Prepositions Quiz

## INTRO
Welcome to the English Quiz Challenge. Today: in, on or at. Three tiny words that confuse almost every English learner.
Here's a quick rule to help you. For time: in for months, years and long periods; on for days and dates; at for exact times. For places: in for inside a space, on for a surface, at for a point or a place where something happens.
Twenty questions, five seconds each. Keep your score!

## QUIZ | Questions 1 to 10
@ q My birthday is ___ June. | *in | on | at
In June. Months take in.
@ q The meeting starts ___ nine o'clock. | in | on | *at
At nine o'clock. Exact times take at.
@ q What do you usually do ___ the weekend? | in | *at | by
At the weekend, in British English. Americans say on the weekend. Both are fine, but in the weekend is wrong.
@ q I was born ___ 1998. | *in | on | at
In 1998. Years take in.
@ q The shop is closed ___ Sundays. | in | *on | at
On Sundays. Days take on.
@ q We have a party ___ the fifth of May. | in | *on | at
On the fifth of May. Dates take on.
@ q I always drink coffee ___ the morning. | *in | on | at
In the morning, in the afternoon, in the evening.
@ q I'll call you ___ Monday morning. | in | *on | at
On Monday morning. When a day comes first, we use on.
@ q The shops are busy ___ Christmas. | in | on | *at
At Christmas, for the holiday period. But on Christmas Day, because it's a day.
@ q I'll be back ___ ten minutes. | *in | on | at
In ten minutes means ten minutes from now.

## QUIZ | Questions 11 to 20
@ q The keys are ___ my bag. | *in | on | at
In my bag: inside a space.
@ q There's a picture ___ the wall. | in | *on | at
On the wall: on a surface.
@ q She's sitting ___ the bus, near the window. | in | *on | at
On the bus. We use on for buses, trains and planes. But in a car or in a taxi.
@ q I'll meet you ___ the bus stop. | in | on | *at
At the bus stop: a point where we meet.
@ q He lives ___ Mumbai. | *in | on | at
In Mumbai. Cities and countries take in.
@ q My flat is ___ the third floor. | in | *on | at
On the third floor.
@ q She works ___ a bank. | in | on | *at
At a bank, at the office, at the hospital: places where you do something. In a bank is also possible when you mean inside the building.
@ q I'm reading an article ___ the internet. | in | *on | at
On the internet, on TV, on the radio.
@ q I can't sleep ___ night. | in | on | *at
At night. But in the morning, in the afternoon, in the evening. Night is the exception.
@ q Turn left ___ the end of the street. | in | on | *at
At the end of the street: a point.
