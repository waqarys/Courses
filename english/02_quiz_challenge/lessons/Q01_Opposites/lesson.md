# Opposites Quiz

## INTRO
Welcome to the English Quiz Challenge. Can you score twenty out of twenty? Today: opposites.
You will see a word and three or four choices. Pick the opposite before the timer ends. If you need more time, pause the video. Keep your score on paper: one point for each correct answer.
The questions get harder as we go. Ready? Let's start.

## QUIZ | Questions 1 to 10
@ q What is the opposite of **hot**? | warm | *cold | wet
Hot and cold. A hot cup of tea, a cold glass of water.
@ q What is the opposite of **full**? | *empty | heavy | open
Full and empty. The fridge was full on Monday, and empty by Friday!
@ q What is the opposite of **early**? | slow | *late | after
Early and late. I arrived early, but my friend arrived late.
@ q What is the opposite of **cheap**? | *expensive | rich | strong
Cheap and expensive. The market is cheap, the mall is expensive.
@ q What is the opposite of **noisy**? | empty | *quiet | soft
Noisy and quiet. The street is noisy in the day and quiet at night.
@ q What is the opposite of **generous**? | kind | poor | *mean
Generous and mean. A generous person shares; a mean person doesn't want to give anything. Careful: mean has other meanings too.
@ q What is the opposite of **borrow**? | *lend | take | return
Borrow and lend. I borrow money from you; you lend money to me.
@ q What is the opposite of **arrive**? | come | *leave | stay
Arrive and leave. The train arrives at six and leaves at ten past six.
@ q What is the opposite of **tight**? | short | *loose | thin
Tight and loose. These jeans are too tight; I need a looser pair.
@ q What is the opposite of **remember**? | know | *forget | think
Remember and forget. Don't forget your keys!

## QUIZ | Questions 11 to 20
@ q What is the opposite of **dangerous**? | *safe | careful | strong
Dangerous and safe. Swimming here is dangerous; the pool is safe.
@ q What is the opposite of **temporary**? | short | *permanent | sudden
Temporary and permanent. A temporary job lasts a few weeks; a permanent job has no end date.
@ q What is the opposite of **succeed**? | win | *fail | finish
Succeed and fail. If you fail the first time, try again, and you'll succeed.
@ q What is the opposite of **ancient**? | old | *modern | short
Ancient and modern. Ancient buildings are thousands of years old; modern ones are new.
@ q What is the opposite of **rough**? | hard | *smooth | dry
Rough and smooth. Sandpaper is rough; glass is smooth.
@ q What is the opposite of **increase**? | *decrease | raise | grow
Increase and decrease. Prices increase; sometimes, they decrease.
@ q What is the opposite of **asleep**? | tired | *awake | alive
Asleep and awake. Are you awake? It's nearly noon!
@ q What is the opposite of **accept**? | agree | *refuse | receive
Accept and refuse. She accepted the job offer. He refused it.
@ q What is the opposite of **shallow**? | *deep | wide | low
Shallow and deep. The shallow end of the pool is for beginners; the deep end is for good swimmers.
@ q What is the opposite of **public**? | open | *private | personal
Public and private. A public park is for everyone; a private garden is only for the owner.
