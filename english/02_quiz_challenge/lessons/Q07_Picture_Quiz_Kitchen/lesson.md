# Picture Quiz: 25 Kitchen Things

## INTRO
Welcome to the English Quiz Challenge. Today is a picture quiz. You'll see a picture of something in the kitchen. Choose the right English name before the timer ends.
Twenty-five pictures. Some are easy, some are tricky. Keep your score!

## QUIZ | Pictures 1 to 14
@ q [draw:kettle] What is this called? | jug | *kettle | teapot
A kettle, for boiling water. Shall I put the kettle on?
@ q [🫖] And what is this? | kettle | *teapot | vase
A teapot, for making and serving tea. A kettle boils the water; a teapot holds the tea.
@ q [🍳] What is this called? | pot | *frying pan | tray
A frying pan, flat with a long handle.
@ q [🍴] What are these called? | *a fork and knife | a spoon and fork | chopsticks
A fork and knife. Together with spoons, we call them cutlery.
@ q [🥄] What is this called? | ladle | *spoon | whisk
A spoon.
@ q [🥣] What is this called? | plate | cup | *bowl
A bowl, for soup or cereal.
@ q [🥢] What are these called? | sticks | *chopsticks | skewers
Chopsticks.
@ q [🫙] What is this called? | bottle | *jar | can
A jar, a glass container with a lid. A jar of jam, a jar of honey.
@ q [🍽️] What is this called? | bowl | *plate | tray
A plate.
@ q [🔪] What is this called? | *knife | saw | scissors
A knife.
@ q [🧂] What is this called? | pepper pot | sugar bowl | *salt shaker
A salt shaker. In British English, also a salt cellar.
@ q [🥤] What is this called? | *a cup with a straw | a bottle | a mug
A cup with a straw.
@ q [☕] What is this called? | glass | *cup of coffee | bowl
A cup of coffee. A big cup with no saucer is called a mug.
@ q [🍷] What is this called? | cup | mug | *wine glass
A wine glass.
