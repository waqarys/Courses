# A, An or The? Articles Quiz

## INTRO
Welcome to the English Quiz Challenge. Today: articles, the little words a, an and the.
Quick rules: use a or an for one thing that isn't specific. A before a consonant sound, an before a vowel sound. Use the when we both know which thing, or there is only one. Sometimes we use no article at all.
Twenty questions, five seconds each. Keep your score!

## QUIZ | Questions 1 to 10
@ q I'd like ___ banana, please. | *a | an | the
A banana. Banana starts with a consonant sound, B.
@ q She's ___ engineer. | a | *an | the
An engineer. Engineer starts with a vowel sound.
@ q I'll be back in ___ hour. | a | *an | the
An hour. The H is silent, so hour starts with a vowel sound: our.
@ q ___ sun is very hot today. | A | An | *The
The sun. There's only one sun.
@ q He studies at ___ university in Delhi. | *a | an | the
A university. University starts with a "you" sound, which is a consonant sound.
@ q Can you close ___ door, please? | a | an | *the
The door. We both know which door: the one in this room.
@ q I saw ___ elephant at the zoo. | a | *an | the
An elephant.
@ q I play ___ guitar in a band. | a | an | *the
The guitar. With musical instruments we usually use the.
@ q She doesn't eat ___ meat. | a | the | *no article
No article. Meat here means meat in general.
@ q We had ___ amazing holiday. | a | *an | the
An amazing holiday. The article goes with the next word: amazing starts with a vowel sound.

## QUIZ | Questions 11 to 20
@ q ___ Mount Everest is the highest mountain in the world. | A | The | *no article
No article. Names of single mountains don't take the. But we say the Himalayas, for a group.
@ q My brother is ___ honest man. | a | *an | the
An honest man. Honest has a silent H.
@ q I go to work by ___ bus. | a | the | *no article
No article. By bus, by train, by car.
@ q ___ Amazon is the longest river in South America. | An | *The | no article
The Amazon. Rivers take the.
@ q Can you play ___ piano? | a | an | *the
The piano.
@ q I need ___ umbrella. It's raining. | a | *an | the
An umbrella.
@ q She wants to be ___ doctor. | *a | an | the
A doctor. We use a or an for jobs.
@ q I usually have ___ breakfast at seven. | a | the | *no article
No article. Breakfast, lunch and dinner, in general, don't take an article.
@ q He wears ___ uniform at work. | *a | an | the
A uniform. Uniform starts with a "you" sound.
@ q What's ___ name of this street? | a | an | *the
The name. It's specific: the name of this street.
