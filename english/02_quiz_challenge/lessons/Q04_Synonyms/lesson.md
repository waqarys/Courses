# Synonyms Quiz

## INTRO
Welcome to the English Quiz Challenge. Today: synonyms. A synonym is a word with the same, or nearly the same, meaning. For example, big and large.
Learning synonyms makes your English sound richer, and helps you understand more. Twenty questions, five seconds each. Ready?

## QUIZ | Questions 1 to 10
@ q Which word means the same as **happy**? | *glad | angry | tired
Glad. I'm glad you came.
@ q Which word means the same as **start**? | finish | *begin | stop
Begin. The film begins at seven.
@ q Which word means the same as **quick**? | *fast | heavy | quiet
Fast. She's a fast runner.
@ q Which word means the same as **difficult**? | easy | *hard | soft
Hard. The exam was really hard.
@ q Which word means the same as **purchase**? | sell | *buy | borrow
Buy. Purchase is a more formal word for buy. You see it on receipts and websites.
@ q Which word means the same as **error**? | *mistake | answer | problem
Mistake. Sorry, that was my mistake.
@ q Which word means the same as **reply**? | ask | *answer | repeat
Answer. Please reply to my email means please answer it.
@ q Which word means the same as **exhausted**? | bored | *very tired | sick
Very tired. After the marathon I was exhausted.
@ q Which word means the same as **choose**? | *pick | lose | keep
Pick. Pick a card, any card.
@ q Which word means the same as **enormous**? | tiny | *huge | long
Huge. An elephant is enormous, or huge.

## QUIZ | Questions 11 to 20
@ q Which word means the same as **tiny**? | *very small | very thin | very short
Very small. A tiny insect.
@ q Which word means the same as **intelligent**? | *clever | funny | lazy
Clever. She's a clever student.
@ q Which word means the same as **rapidly**? | slowly | *quickly | carefully
Quickly. Prices are rising rapidly.
@ q Which word means the same as **assist**? | *help | watch | stop
Help. Can I assist you? is a polite way to say can I help you?
@ q Which word means the same as **brave**? | scared | *courageous | proud
Courageous. A brave firefighter.
@ q Which word means the same as **silent**? | *quiet | empty | dark
Quiet. The room went silent.
@ q Which word means the same as **cautious**? | *careful | angry | friendly
Careful. Be cautious when you cross the road.
@ q Which word means the same as **wealthy**? | healthy | *rich | famous
Rich. Careful: wealthy and healthy sound similar, but mean different things.
@ q Which word means the same as **repair**? | break | *fix | clean
Fix. Can you fix my bike?
@ q Which word means the same as **annual**? | monthly | *yearly | daily
Yearly. An annual event happens once a year.
