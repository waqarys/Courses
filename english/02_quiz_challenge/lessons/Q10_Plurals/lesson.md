# Plurals Quiz

## INTRO
Welcome to the English Quiz Challenge. Today: plurals. Usually we just add S: one cat, two cats. But many common words have special plurals.
Twenty questions, five seconds each. Choose the correct plural. Keep your score!

## QUIZ | Questions 1 to 10
@ q [🐭] One mouse, two ___. | mouses | *mice | mices
Mice. One mouse, two mice.
@ q [🔪] One knife, two ___. | knifes | *knives | knive
Knives. Many words ending in F or F-E change to V-E-S: knife, knives; wife, wives; leaf, leaves.
@ q [🦷] One tooth, two ___. | tooths | teeths | *teeth
Teeth.
@ q [🦶] One foot, two ___. | foots | *feet | feets
Feet.
@ q [🥔] One potato, two ___. | potatos | *potatoes | potatoe
Potatoes. Some words ending in O add E-S: potatoes, tomatoes, heroes.
@ q [🚌] One bus, two ___. | buss | *buses | busies
Buses. Words ending in S, X, CH or SH add E-S.
@ q [👶] One baby, two ___. | babys | *babies | babyes
Babies. Consonant plus Y: change Y to I-E-S.
@ q [🧑‍🤝‍🧑] One person, two ___. | persons | *people | peoples
People. Persons exists, but it is very formal.
@ q [🍃] One leaf, many ___. | leafs | *leaves | leafes
Leaves.
@ q [👩] One woman, two ___. | womans | *women | womens
Women. We say it "wimmin".

## QUIZ | Questions 11 to 20
@ q [🔑] One key, two ___. | kies | *keys | keyes
Keys. Vowel plus Y: just add S. Key, keys; day, days.
@ q [📦] One box, two ___. | boxs | *boxes | boxies
Boxes.
@ q [🐟] One fish, many ___. | *fish | fishs | fishies
Fish. The plural is usually the same: one fish, ten fish.
@ q [🐑] One sheep, a hundred ___. | sheeps | *sheep | sheepes
Sheep. Same in the singular and plural, like deer.
@ q [🍒] One cherry, a bowl of ___. | cherrys | *cherries | cherryes
Cherries.
@ q [⌚] One watch, two ___. | watchs | *watches | watchies
Watches. C-H adds E-S.
@ q [🪟] One window, two ___. | windowes | *windows | windowies
Windows. Just add S.
@ q [👨] One man, two ___. | mans | *men | mens
Men.
@ q [🍅] One tomato, a kilo of ___. | tomatos | *tomatoes | tomatoe
Tomatoes.
@ q [🪤] One trap, two ___. | trapes | *traps | trappes
Traps. Most words just add S!
