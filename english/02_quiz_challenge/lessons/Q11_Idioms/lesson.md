# Idioms Quiz

## INTRO
Welcome to the English Quiz Challenge. Today: idioms. An idiom is a group of words with a meaning you can't guess from the single words. If something is a piece of cake, there is no cake!
Twenty common idioms, five seconds each. Choose what it really means. Keep your score!

## QUIZ | Questions 1 to 10
@ q [🍰] The test was **a piece of cake**. | *very easy | delicious | very long
Very easy. Don't worry about the test, it'll be a piece of cake.
@ q [🧊] He told a joke to **break the ice**. | break something | *make people feel relaxed | make people leave
Make people feel relaxed when they first meet.
@ q [🔔] Her name **rings a bell**. | she called me | *it sounds familiar | it's very loud
It sounds familiar. I think I've heard it before.
@ q [⏰] We finished the report **in the nick of time**. | very late | *just in time | too early
Just in time, at the last possible moment.
@ q [🛌] I'm going to **hit the sack**. | *go to bed | go to the gym | get angry
Go to bed. I'm exhausted, I'm going to hit the sack.
@ q [🌧️] I'm feeling a bit **under the weather**. | *a little ill | wet | cold
A little ill. I'm under the weather, so I'm staying home today.
@ q [🐱] Don't **let the cat out of the bag**! | *tell the secret | lose your pet | go outside
Don't tell the secret. It's a surprise party!
@ q [🌙] I eat out **once in a blue moon**. | every night | *very rarely | every month
Very rarely.
@ q [🦵] **Break a leg** in your performance tonight! | be careful | *good luck | slow down
Good luck! It's what people say to actors before a show.
@ q [🧑‍🤝‍🧑] We **see eye to eye** on most things. | *agree | argue | look at each other
Agree. My boss and I see eye to eye.

## QUIZ | Questions 11 to 20
@ q [🎯] You **hit the nail on the head**. | you hurt yourself | *you're exactly right | you worked hard
You're exactly right.
@ q [🤐] **Keep it under your hat**. | wear a hat | *keep it secret | keep it safe
Keep it secret.
@ q [💸] That watch **costs an arm and a leg**. | *it's very expensive | it's dangerous | it's broken
It's very expensive.
@ q [🧠] I'll **sleep on it**. | *I'll decide tomorrow | I'm tired | I'll forget
I'll think about it overnight and decide tomorrow.
@ q [🐦] Let's **kill two birds with one stone**. | go hunting | *do two things at once | be cruel
Do two things with one action. I'll go to the bank and buy groceries on the way: two birds, one stone.
@ q [🥶] Two days before the wedding, he got **cold feet**. | *he felt nervous and wanted to stop | he needed socks | he was ill
Feeling nervous about a big event and wanting to stop.
@ q [🍎] She's **the apple of his eye**. | *he loves her very much | she likes fruit | she's beautiful
He loves her very much.
@ q [⛵] We're all **in the same boat**. | on holiday | *in the same difficult situation | travelling together
In the same difficult situation.
@ q [🎂] Their new café **took the cake** for the best coffee in town. Which meaning fits? | *was the best | ate the cake | was expensive
Was the best. Take the cake means be the best, or sometimes, the most surprising.
@ q [👂] **I'm all ears.** | I can't hear | *I'm listening carefully | I'm tired
I'm listening carefully. Tell me everything, I'm all ears.
