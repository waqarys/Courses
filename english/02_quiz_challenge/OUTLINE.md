# Series 2: English Quiz Challenge: Can You Score 20/20? (12 lessons, season 1)

Teen and adult learners, A2-B1. NOT made for kids. Each quiz: intro with the rule of the day, 20 multiple-choice
questions (25 in the picture quiz, 30 in the finale) in two halves, 5-second countdown, reveal with a ding, the
answer read aloud plus a one-line reason or example, a halfway score check (subscribe ask), and a grade at the end
("write your score in the comments"). Pin a bonus question as a comment after upload. Estimated 7-12 min each.
36 Shorts (one question each, timer, reveal).
